`spec×spec-error-by-orchestrator` is new — and it is the largest single
source of blocking findings this cascade (stalled dispatch on a doubled UNC

## Proposals

Adopted alone, advisory detection rules, drafted as decisions:

- **D-008** every id reaching a write is containment-checked, by resolved-path
  relation, never `startsWith`.
- **D-009** redaction and escaping at the write boundary of every tracked
  file, not only receipts and ci logs.
- **D-010** a test that cannot fail is not evidence; the mutation step stays
  mandatory.
- **D-011** a file duplicating manifest or docs data is rendered, and its
  drift detected.

Referred to the Patron as one consolidated petitio, **P-001** (`needs_you`):
builder isolation as a lex clause; `opus.untracked` as a blocking rule;
`opus.red_evidence` as a blocking rule; and promotion of D-008 to blocking
after one clean cascade. Clause 2 is the one to take first.
