1. **W-139**, record-only CI runs the tests that read the real officina.
   Every checkpoint and record PR takes the fast path, and one has already
   turned master red (#218). It is the smallest item here and it protects
   every merge that follows.

   trunk. The predicate (`trunkContainsMerge`) already landed with
   W-124/W-128, so this is a small guard on the one verb that can still close
   unmerged work.

   sets the shape of every brief written after it, W-126's and W-120's
   included. Settling the brief shape first gives W-126 a stable thing to
   cite.

   review rounds on every later opus, and it reads best against W-127's
   numbered behaviours. W-124's filed follow-on (the review cites the
   sec-lead input it used) depends on it.

   blocking finding. It is one check rule that nothing else waits on. It
   also covers P-014 and P-020.
