## Intent

A reader of CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md should learn from the Roles section who signs a collegium's specs, not only who its magister is. Today the section prints the magister key alone, and for design that key is a legacy signer id, so the line points a reader at the wrong seat.

1. **The Roles line names the spec signer (behaviour 1).** `rolesSection` in `packages/cli/src/instructions.ts` renders, for each collegium in `bisellium.yml#collegia`, `- <id> (magister: <magister>; spec signer: <signer>)` when `spec_seats` routes a new, non-UI opus of that collegium to a signer, and `- <id> (magister: <magister>)` as today otherwise. The signer is read through `readSpecSeats` (`packages/commands/src/spec-seats.ts`), the one reader `next` and `verdict --phase spec` share, called unchanged with a record holding only the collegium; so the line never names a signer the routing would refuse, and the section holds no second copy of the spec_seats rules (lesson class indirection-duplicates-source-of-truth).
2. **The committed generated files are the new render (behaviour 2).** CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are regenerated by `bisellium instructions --studio studio --repo . --write` (`apply-b2.py` runs it; GLOSSARY.md renders unchanged), and `w589.test.ts` joins `test:suite` and `test:record`.

**Every renderer of a Roles line or a collegium's signer (census, `importers.sh` and `grep magister` over packages, apps, adapters and scripts):** (a) `rolesSection` → CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md (`renderInstructions`, `instructions`, `instructions.stale`): closed (behaviours 1 and 2); (b) `init` (`packages/cli/src/init.ts`) writes CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md through `renderInstructions` for a fresh studio: closed through it (its scaffolded manifest has no `spec_seats`, so it renders as today); (c) GLOSSARY.md: names no collegium, unchanged; (d) `bisellium context` (`packages/commands/src/context.ts`): renders no magister or signer line, no site; (e) the console's `/api/officina` (`apps/server/src/store.ts`) returns each collegium's `magister` and no screen renders it (`apps/web/src/screens/`): a stated limit, the route's shape unchanged; (f) the lex headers (`studio/leges/*.md`, hand prose, code-owned): a stated limit, unchanged (design's already names the design lead); (g) the dossier and `docs/research/**` snapshots of AGENTS.md: frozen documents, a stated limit. The readers of `magister` that route work (`next.ts`, `verdict.ts`, `retro.ts`, `brief-stale.ts`, `lifecycle.ts`, `tick.ts`, the native adapter) are not renderers and are unchanged (W-590, held).
