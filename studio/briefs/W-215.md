## Intent

Decree family: convergent-review

Today each review round is a fresh session judging the whole subject: spec reviews took 65 rounds over 15 opera (42 failed, mean 4.3, max 12), build reviews 205 over 88 (mean 2.3, max 8), the W-205 handoff 15. The Patron's four parts (2026-10-11), and the mechanism each gets:

1. **One reviewer session.** `verdict --session <id>` records `# session:`. A sequence is one opus, one phase (`spec`, `build`, `handoff`) and one sella. Once a sequence records a session, the writer refuses a later verdict of that sequence with another session, or none, unless `--session-reason` states why it was replaced.
2. **Changed lines and class closure.** From the sequence's second sessioned round, `scripts/review-packet.mjs` gives the resumed reviewer `delta.diff`, the change since its previous round. Its verdict carries `## Earlier findings`, one line per standing blocker of that previous round: `closed`, `open` or `reversed`. The writer refuses a verdict that misses one.
3. **Facts as given.** The same script writes `facts.txt` before the round, from code: `next` per opus, brief admission, and for a handoff W-186's `sessionHandoffFindings` plus `next` and admission for each opus on an added line of the delta (a deleted line states nothing left to check).
4. **Dispute before failure.** The author sends a `## Rebuttal` with evidence into the same session. The reviewer answers once, in `## Ruling`: withdrawn, or upheld with a citation. Both sections are recorded in the round's verdict log. A withdrawn blocker no longer stands, so the round passes if nothing else stands. A ruling the reviewer cannot make with a citation is not recordable; the dispute goes to the Patron, whose answer is recorded as the ruling.

The measurement is code: `reviewRounds` re-measures the baseline from committed verdict logs and counts rebuttals, withdrawals and reversals. Every `retro --opus` acta prints it, and `scripts/review-rounds.mjs` prints the same lines on demand.

D-014 (the censor is the single gate), D-053 (no round cap), D-054 (judge changed lines) and D-056 (only substance blocks) stand. No round cap is added. W-216 (agents ask each other) is not specified here; the exchange is a section of the verdict log, so the retro reads it. The threat model is mistakes, not adversaries (D-045).

Promise one-session: once a verdict of a sequence records a session, each later verdict of that sequence records the same session, or a different one with a recorded reason — limit: the writer trusts the id it is given, so a fresh session given the old id passes (D-045); a sequence whose verdicts record no session is unconstrained; the measurement classifies by opus and phase, so such a sequence counts as before unless another sella's rounds in the same opus and phase carry a session, and then it counts as after.

Promise changed-lines: from a sequence's second sessioned round, the reviewer is given the change since that round's predecessor and must mark each of the predecessor's standing blockers closed, open or reversed — limit: the writer checks that every standing blocker is accounted for once, not that the judgment kept to changed lines or that a `closed` is true; the spec and handoff delta is taken against the subject copy the script saved in the predecessor's packet, which it trusts; a build delta starts at the commit that added the predecessor's log, so a source commit made between a review and its recording is outside the delta.

Promise computed-facts: `facts.txt` holds, for each fact, the command or function, its output and, for a command, its exit, computed before the reviewer runs — limit: facts are as of packet time; nothing makes the reviewer read them; `next`'s output depends on the network for a pushed branch's PR state, and whatever it prints is recorded as the fact.

Promise dispute-first: a sessioned verdict that records a dispute records exactly one rebuttal and one ruling, every disputed finding is ruled withdrawn or upheld with a file:line citation, and a withdrawn finding does not stand — limit: the code checks the shape and the citation's form, not that the citation proves the point; an `open` ruling is refused, so a dispute the Patron has not answered cannot be recorded at all; the whole exchange must fit the 8192-byte body cap (W-131).

Promise measured: W-215's `scripts/review-rounds.mjs`, run on the `studio/` of a checkout of master 36ca84cb, prints `rounds.txt` byte for byte, then totals, before, after and target lines; each `retro --opus` acta carries the after and target lines with its own opus's counts — limit: a reversal the reviewer does not mark `reversed` is not counted; a withdrawal at ruling is counted apart and does not miss the no-reversal target; the handoff baseline (15 rounds) has no committed logs and stays cited in the Patron's acta, not computed.

## Interfaces

```text

```

`--phase handoff` writes `ci/<opus>-handoff-<n>.log`, `# phase: handoff`, with the non-UI findings check on every opus kind, no `# brief:` pin, no conversion, and no UI flag. `# session:` and `# session_reason:` follow `# model:`.

The sequence and its predecessor: `readSequence(root, opus, phase, sella)` (new) returns the highest-round `ci/<opus>-<spec|review|handoff>-<m>.log` whose header block's `# sella:` is `sella`, its session if any, and, when it has one, its `parseVerdictLog` result. The opus id reaches every path through `safeItemPath` and every read through `readContainedRegularFile`, as in `runVerdict` today. A verdict carrying `--session S`, or whose predecessor carries a session P:

- P present: the round is above the predecessor's; `--session` is required; S ≠ P needs `--session-reason`; S = P refuses it.
- P absent: `--session` is optional and `--session-reason` is refused.
- With S: the outcome is exactly `passed` or `failed`; the log to be written parses with `parseVerdictLog`; `failed` iff a finding stands (blocking, not converted, not withdrawn); an `open` earlier line only beside `failed`; with P, `## Earlier findings` names each standing blocker of the predecessor once and nothing else.

Body sections (`readExchange`, new; each exact heading at most once, running to the next `## ` line; blank lines skipped; CITE is a `path:line` token, `[\w./-]+:[1-9]\d*`):

```text
## Rebuttal
<k>. <evidence holding a CITE: a file:line, or facts.txt:<line> for a command's output>
## Ruling
<k>. withdrawn — <why>
<k>. upheld — <why, holding a CITE>
## Earlier findings
- ci/<opus>-<kind>-<m>.log #<k>: closed — <evidence>
- ci/<opus>-<kind>-<m>.log #<k>: open — <evidence>
- ci/<opus>-<kind>-<m>.log #<k>: reversed — <why, holding a CITE>
```

Each rebuttal and ruling `<k>` is a finding of this log, once each; the two sections come together and rule the same set. ` - ` is accepted for ` — `. A withdrawn finding parses as `blocking: false, withdrawn: true`, so `readSpecReview`, `parseBuildReviewLog` and `readOpusVerdicts` see it as not standing, unchanged.

`next`'s orders (`reviewSession(src, opus, kind, sella)`, new: the newest `ci/<opus>-<kind>-<m>.log` by `sella`, read with `parseLogHeader`; spec from the working tree, build from the opus branch):
