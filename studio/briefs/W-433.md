## Revision 2

The round 2 review found one blocker (S1, `spec×input-domain-missing-or-unenforceable`): the new reader did not share one fail-closed record source. Revision 2 closes both paths:

- **One record.** `uiSpecProblems` and `uiSpecInput` judge the input against the spec owner's record, the one `ownerUi`, the seat pairing and the verdict writer already read (the main checkout's working record before the branch, the usable worktree's after it). ## Intent

Two dead ends on a `kind: ui` opus's spec, both in the ui-lead's input (W-338, D-088 E), one opus because they are one decree: a ui-lead input that asks for a revision is real evidence and is acted on.

1. **A ui-lead revise cannot be recorded once its sequence carries a session.** A review sequence is one opus, one phase and one sella (W-215, `readSequence`). Once a ui-lead round records `# session:`, a later round with no `--session` is refused ("ci/W-407-spec-10.log records session …; a later verdict of the sequence needs --session"), and one with `--session` is judged by `sessionedProblem`, whose first line refuses every outcome but `passed` and `failed` ("a sessioned verdict's outcome is exactly passed or failed"). A ui-lead outcome is `passed`, `failed` or `revise` (W-338), so a sessioned `revise` has no recordable form. The ui-lead runs a fresh read-only session each round by design, so it records `--session <its own id>` with `--session-reason`. Live cases: W-407 round 13 (`ui-input-13.md`, one blocking finding, Codex session `01a17ff4-bdc9-7151-8def-0d82e5a67f61`, the sequence's session `01a17dbd-…` from round 10) and W-408 round 6 (`ui-input-6.md`, one blocking finding, session `01a1808c-3f0a-72f3-bebb-a963fb35b1b2`) (`enumerate.out`).
2. **A recorded ui-lead revise does not stop the branch.** `authoritativeUiInput` admits the newest ui-lead log on the current design digest whatever its outcome, so once the signature and review pass, the spec rung of the ladder names the branch over a current `revise` or `failed` input and its blocking findings. This still holds on master: row `W-433-b2` fails there (`master-b2-r2.out`, and its red), and W-415 reached done with its last ui-lead input `revise` with three blocking findings (`enumerate.out`, `ci/W-415-spec-6.log`).

The rule, kept that small:

- The ui-lead's UI spec input, and only it, may record `revise` on a sessioned sequence. It carries its own `--session`, with `--session-reason` when it differs from the sequence's, exactly as any other replaced session. It is judged as a `failed` round is: it needs a standing blocking finding, and the next round of the sequence accounts for that blocker under `## Earlier findings` (W-215's rule, unchanged). Nothing else loosens: every other seat's sessioned outcome stays `passed` or `failed`, and the session and reason rules are unchanged.
- A current ui-lead input recorded `revise` or `failed` orders the signer at the next round, never the branch. The signer revises the brief and signs again; the edit makes the input's design digest stale, so the review and then the ui-lead are ordered again on the new brief (W-338's ladder, unchanged).
- The input is read only where the spec is: against the spec owner's record, in the main checkout before the branch and in the usable opus worktree after it. With the branch and no usable worktree, a `kind: ui` spec holds at spec. Once the PR is settled none of the spec's UI orders fires.

Threat model: mistakes, not adversaries (D-045). The writer trusts the session id it is given, as W-215 states.
