## Interfaces

`findingTagProblems(body: string, phase: "spec" | "build"): string[]` (new, exported from `verdict.ts`, text only): one problem per finding and rule, in finding order. `runVerdict` calls it for a non-UI spec or build verdict, right after the findings-shape check and before the brief, ledger, sequence and ticket checks; each problem prints as `<id>: finding tags: <problem>`, and the writer exits 2 with no log written and the ticket unclaimed. A handoff verdict and a kind: ui opus's verdicts are not read. The problems:

1. `finding <n> carries <k> [severity:] tags; one only`, for any finding.
2. `finding <n> has no [severity: S1|S2|S3|S4] tag; every build finding carries one (D-085 C)`, for a build finding.
3. `finding <n> is blocking and tagged [severity: S<k>]; only an S1 blocks: reissue it as advisory, or fix the tag (D-086 C)`, for a blocking spec or build finding tagged S2, S3 or S4.
4. `finding <n> is a blocking spec finding with no [design: changes|keeps] tag; it carries exactly one (D-086 A)` (or `with <k> … tags`), for a blocking spec finding.

The forms `findingTagProblems` recognises, one per line (the grammar lines of Input domain row 1):

- a finding: a line of the one Findings section as `contentLines` reads it (fences and HTML comments are not lines), starting with a positive number, a dot, a space and a non-space — pinned by `W-297-b1` "three findings, three problems"
- blocking: the finding opens `blocking` after its number, in any case, with or without `**` before it — pinned by `W-297-b1` "a bold build blocker tagged S1"
- a severity tag: exactly `[severity: S1]`, `[severity: S2]`, `[severity: S3]` or `[severity: S4]`, anywhere on the line — pinned by `W-297-b1` "the form" and pinned by `W-297-b1` "two severity tags"
- a design tag: exactly `[design: changes]` or `[design: keeps]`, anywhere on the line — pinned by `W-297-b1` "the design form" and pinned by `W-297-b1` "two design tags"

Every other form is a limit, and it reads as no tag, so a required tag written in it is refused: another case, spacing or bracket (`[severity:S1]`, `[Severity: S1]`, `(severity: S1)`), an `S5`, a tag split over lines, and a tag in a fence or a comment. A tag inside the finding's prose (a quoted replacement text) is read as the finding's own.

`designOf(text: string): "changes" | "keeps" | undefined` (new, exported): the one design tag of a finding's text, or undefined for none or several.

`readSpecReview` (W-162) also returns `keeps`: each standing blocker as `<log> #<n>` when the review failed and every standing blocker's `designOf` is keeps, else empty (a converted or withdrawn blocker does not stand, as today). In `reviewedSpec`, a sequence whose newest review is such a review is the new gate `{ kind: "keeps"; review; fixes }`: the spec rung is passed, with the why `<review> failed <signature> only on blockers that keep the design: they are required build fixes (D-086 A)`. `deriveNext` then adds one line `required-fixes: <log> #<n>, …` to the branch order and to the reds, build and review dispatch orders while the gate is keeps. The build packet's facts hold the next verb's output (`nextFact`, unchanged), so the review packet lists them.

The hold, in the build rung after `boundaryExtra`, only when the newest round failed at this tree and brief (`failedNow`): `classLoop(f, rounds)` returns a reason and `deriveNext` returns `held` at step build, actor producer, no order, exit 1, with the why `<round m> and <round n> failed on class <class> against one brief` or `<round l>, <round m> and <round n> failed in a row`, then `: a class loop goes to the architect, not a builder (D-085): state the limit in briefs/<id>.md (the forms the rule recognises, every other form a limit) and sign it again in .worktrees/<id>/studio, or sign it unchanged when the form is inside the stated domain; a security or evidence blocker opens blocking (security) or blocking (evidence)`. The forms `classLoop` recognises, one per line (the grammar lines of Input domain row 3):

- a loop of one class: the newest two rounds failed, both carry one `# brief:` value, and some standing blocker of each carries one `[class: <area>×<slug>]` tag (area and slug each a lower-case word of letters, digits and hyphens) — pinned by `W-297-b3` "two rounds sharing a class against one brief" and pinned by `W-297-b3` "one class across two briefs"
- a run of three: the newest three rounds failed, whatever their classes — pinned by `W-297-b3` "three failed rounds of three classes" and pinned by `W-297-b3` "a passed round between two of one class"
- an exempt round: a standing blocker of the newest round opens exactly `blocking (security)` or `blocking (evidence)`, in any case, with either zero or two leading asterisks (two closed by two right after the parenthesis, `**blocking (security)**`), or carries one well-formed `[class: security×<slug>]` tag (the class form above, closed); malformed emphasis (`*blocking (security)`, `***blocking (evidence)`) and a malformed or incomplete security class (`[class: security×]`, an unclosed `[class: security×x`) are not exempt — pinned by `W-297-b3` "is exempt" and pinned by `W-297-b3` "is not exempt"
- the answer: a valid architect signature (`readSpecSignature`, the design magister, `passed`, no standing blocker, its own `# brief:` pin) among `ci/<id>-spec-<n>.log` in opus/<id>'s ci/ (the worktree's when usable, else the branch's), whose `# at:` is after the newest round's — pinned by `W-297-b3` "a newer architect signature releases the fix" and pinned by `W-297-b3` "a newer spec verdict by another sella"

Every other form is a limit: a class tag in another case or spacing, a class shared only through an advisory or a converted blocker, a missing-evidence or security blocker written as plain `blocking` with another class or with its emphasis malformed (it holds, never exempts), and a signature whose `# at:` is earlier than the round it answers.

Promise refuses: the verdict writer records a non-UI spec or build verdict only when each build finding carries one severity tag, no blocking finding is tagged S2, S3 or S4, and each blocking spec finding carries one design tag, as the forms above read them — limit: the tags are read as words; whether the severity or the design tag is right stays the reviewer's (D-066 C), and a handoff verdict is not read.

Promise keeps-branch: a reviewer-configured opus whose newest spec review failed only on blockers that keep the design passes the spec rung, and the next orders through review name each of those blockers as a required fix — limit: the orders name them; whether a build round closes them stays the build reviewer's until part 2's later-round check, and a later signature (a re-spec, or the answer to a hold) followed by a passed review ends the list.

Promise loop-hold: a build fix is held, never dispatched, while the newest two failed rounds share a class against one brief or the newest three failed, unless the newest round has an exempt blocker or an architect signature newer than it exists in the opus's ci/ — limit: the hold reads the tags reviewers write; a loop on untagged blockers holds only at the third failed round, and the hold is routing, not a cap (D-053 stands): any number of rounds may follow an answer.

Limits, stated:

- Recorded logs are not re-judged: the writer reads only new verdicts, and the next verb reads a recorded blocker with no design tag as not keeps, so every recorded failed spec review keeps today's route. 22 of the 86 spec and build logs recorded since 2026-10-17 lack a tag the writer now requires (`census.out`); after this lands each such verdict is reissued once by the same reviewer session (D-086 C).
- Unchanged code the promises lean on is a stated limit, not a guarantee (D-076 E): `contentLines`, `parseVerdictLog` (the converted and withdrawn readings), `readSpecSignature`, `ownBrief`, `failedSpecReviews` and D-061 §4's split, `readBuildRounds`, `reviewGate`, `parseLogHeader`, `instant`, `nextFact` in `scripts/review-packet.mjs`, and `runVerdict`'s refusal order.
- The review prompts (`studio/prompts/`) do not name `blocking (security)` or `blocking (evidence)`; until part 2's prompt edit a reviewer writes them only when told, and an untagged security or evidence blocker in a loop holds until the architect signs it unchanged, which releases it.
