## Intent

W-302's spec review round 2 failed three blockers on one sentence shape (`studio/ci/W-302-spec-2.log` on `opus/W-302`). Revision 1 made one promise in three places, the status line, Acceptance and a Default: `The diff is the Files owned.`, or the same confined to `.github/`. That cannot hold: the verbs write the opus record, the `studio/ci/` logs, the reds and receipts into the same opus history, and none of them is owned. Class `spec×promise-unattainable-under-accepted-domain`: 18 lessons under `studio/lessons/` (L-089 to L-197; the opus title's count of 11 is older). W-197's round 2 failed on the same line (L-109). The reviewer has also passed the same line many times (W-243, W-255, W-293), so the review alone is an uneven gate. D-070 C asks for a cause fix; this is a brief-admission rule (D-039 high).

After this opus, in an officina that declares `brief_behaviour_limit` (this studio declares 6), brief admission refuses such a sentence unless its line scopes it to the source diff or names the officina evidence. `readBriefAdmission` is the one place, so in such an officina `ready`, `check`'s `brief.admission` (active opera) and the `next` gates that call it (W-196's review gate, W-255's light-brief gates) read the same problem. Where the key is absent, `ready`, `check` and `next` run no full-brief admission today, so this rule does not run either (Out of scope: W-264). The refusal quotes the standard line, which W-302's revision 2 used:

> The source diff (every path outside `studio/`) touches only the files in Files owned. The officina's evidence is written by the verbs and is not owned: the opus record, `studio/ci/` logs, `studio/ci/reds/<id>/` and receipts.

`bisellium new --brief`, the one spec template in the tree, writes that line under `## Acceptance` with the opus id. The builder-brief and spec-draft templates do not exist on master: W-241 part 1 adds them under `studio/prompts/` (Defaults 2).

The detector is a narrow textual rule, in the manner of W-196's absolute phrases: three closed phrase lists, one line at a time. Its ceilings are named under Interfaces. It does not parse what a sentence means.

Threat model: mistakes, not adversaries (D-045).

## Interfaces

`readBriefAdmission(briefText, limit)` keeps its signed two-parameter signature (`W-140-b3`). After `absolutePromiseProblems`, and before the brief-form error, it appends `wholeDiffProblems(real)`, where `real` is `realLines`' output: fenced blocks and HTML comments blanked, one entry per file line.

`wholeDiffProblems` reads each real line once:

1. A line whose raw text matches the scope list is skipped. The scope list (case-insensitive): `source diff`, `source change`, `outside studio` or `outside the studio`, with or without a backtick before `studio`, and `officina evidence` or `officina's evidence`.
2. Otherwise the line is split into sentences after `.`, `!`, `?` or `;` followed by whitespace, and at every `|` (table cells). Each sentence has its inline code removed (`withoutInlineCode`, the W-196 helper).
3. A sentence that holds a whole-diff subject and a confinement is one problem; at most one per line. Subjects (case-insensitive, whole words): `the diff`, `the whole diff`, `the entire diff`, `the full diff`, `the whole change`, `the entire change`, `the full change`, `every file`, `every changed file`, `all files`, `all changed files`, `all the files`, `all the changed files`. Confinements: `Files owned` (any case), the word `only`, or `is`, `are`, `lies` or `stays`, optionally followed by `all` or `entirely`, then `under`, `inside` or `within`.

The problem, which `ready` prints as `<id>: brief.admission: <spec> <problem>` and `check` reports as a `brief.admission` block:

`line <n> confines "<subject as written>" to Files owned or one folder, but the verbs also write officina evidence; scope it to the source diff or name that evidence as not owned: "<SOURCE_DIFF_LINE>"`

`SOURCE_DIFF_LINE` is the standard line above, with the literal `<id>`. `briefTemplate(id, title)` writes `## Acceptance` as `TODO.`, a blank line, then `- ` and `SOURCE_DIFF_LINE` with `<id>` replaced by the new id; the other five sections stay `TODO.`.

What the rule catches, from W-302 revision 1: `Every file this opus changes outside the test is under .github/` (subject `every file`, confinement `is under`), `The diff is the Files owned.` and `The whole change is under .github/ apart from one test line`. Also `the diff stays within Files owned`, `All changed files are under packages/`, and `The entire diff touches only scripts/`.

What it does not catch (false negatives):

- A promise wrapped across two lines. Older hard-wrapped briefs (W-046 style) are not read as one sentence.
- Subjects outside the list: `the change`, `the PR`, `this opus touches`, `nothing outside`.
- Confinements outside the list: `is limited to`, `is confined to`, `in x/ alone`.
- A line that holds a scope phrase for another reason: the whole line is skipped.
- A promise inside inline code, a fence or a comment, by design: that is how a brief quotes the bad wording.

What it refuses that may be true (false positives): a promise that already covers the evidence in other words, such as `the diff touches only Files owned and studio/`, or `red logs aside`; and a sentence that names the diff and uses `only` about something else, such as `the diff is read only once`. The remedy for both is the standard line. The census found neither shape among the 141 briefs on master.

The scope list names the literal `studio`, the real officina's directory. A brief in an officina with another directory name scopes itself with `source diff`. The template writes the same literal line in every officina.
