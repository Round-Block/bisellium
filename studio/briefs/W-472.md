## Intent

An opus title is at most 8 words. The long text goes in the record's description, which the Board drawer shows.

**Where the description lives: the record's body.** `docs/ADOPTION.md` already defines it: everything after the front-matter delimiter is the description, preserved byte for byte by metadata writers, and there is no `summary:` or `description:` field. The served API already carries it: `/api/opera` and `/api/opus/<id>` return each record's `body` (`apps/server/src/store.ts`, `readOpusBody`: authored bytes up to 64 KiB, the final line ending dropped as file framing), and the Board drawer already renders it in full under "From the officina record" (`apps/web/src/screens/BoardDrawer.tsx`, from `lib/board.ts`'s `drawerDetail`), with the title unclamped in its header. So no new field, no new vocabulary and no screen change: what is missing is a writer for the body and the cap. `new` gains `--description <text>`, which writes the text verbatim below the front matter.

**What a word is.** A whitespace-separated token (`\s`, Unicode) holding a letter or a number of any script (`\p{L}`, `\p{N}`): `W-463's`, `2-line` and `10-30` are one word each, and a lone `—`, `(` or `:` is none, so a title is not charged for its punctuation.

**What enforces the cap: the writers, and a check rule that advises.** Every writer that files or retitles an opus refuses a longer title before any write: `new` (`runNew` and `newItem`), `retro --opus` (its triage pre-check, ahead of the `newItem` it calls) and `amend --title`. `check` advises `opus.title.words` on a record that is not `done` whose title is longer, never blocks, and says nothing on a done record (its title is history).

**Existing records: advised, not migrated.** A migration cannot be mechanical: a short title is a summary someone writes, and the probe shows a mechanical cut (the text before the first `: `, ` (`, `; `, ` — ` or `, `) gives 1 to 8 words for only 323 of the 439, some of them meaningless (`W-034: done`; `title-prefix-probe.out`). Retitling also costs: a title amended after a signed spec makes `brief.stale` ask the architect to re-sign, and a kind: ui record's `design_digest` binds its exact title, so its UI input goes stale. So the old records stay valid and carry the advisory until they are done; any one of them is shortened by `amend --title`, which keeps the old title in the record's `amendments` (`superseded:`). A one-off retitle of the open records is a follow-up for the Patron (Defaults 3).

**Contract.** The cap and `--description` are a native-model contract change, so `docs/ADOPTION.md` (code-owned) states them in its title paragraph and its `new` synopsis line, with no line added (it is at its `doc.cap`): the Patron merges.

## Interfaces

**`TITLE_MAX_WORDS = 8`**, **`titleWordCount(title: string): number`** (the tokens of `title.trim().split(/\s+/u)` matching `/[\p{L}\p{N}]/u`) and **`titleWordsProblem(value: unknown): string | undefined`**: `title has <n> words, at most 8; put the rest in the description` when a string has more than 8 words; undefined for a string of at most 8 and for a non-string (that is `titleProblem`'s). Exported from `@bisellium/commands/opus-model.js`.

**`descriptionProblem(value: string): string | undefined`**: `description must be nonblank; omit --description for none` for a value that trims to empty; `description contains forbidden control U+<hex>` for a C0 or C1 control (DEL included) other than TAB and LF; else undefined.

**`new`**: `bisellium new --kind <kind> --collegium <collegium> --title <title> [--arc <id>] [--parent <id>] [--spec <path>] [--description <text>] [--brief] [dir]`. A title over the cap: stderr `opus.title.words: <titleWordsProblem>`, exit 1, nothing written. A bad description: stderr `--description: <descriptionProblem>`, exit 1, nothing written. A good one: the record's bytes are its front matter, then the description verbatim, then one LF unless it already ends in one. `newItem` returns `{ ok: false, message: "opus.title.words: <titleWordsProblem>" }` for a title over the cap.

**`retro --opus`**: a triage fix `{ title, collegium }` whose title is over the cap is refused as `triage: fix of "<class>": <titleWordsProblem>`, exit 2, before any write.

**`amend --title`**: a title over the cap: stderr `amend: --title <titleWordsProblem>`, exit 2, nothing written.

**`check`**: `opus.title.words`, level advise, at `opera/<id>.md`, for a record whose `state` is not `done` and whose title `titleWordsProblem` refuses: `<titleWordsProblem> (W-472): shorten it with bisellium amend --title`.

Promise writers: every writer that files an opus refuses a title of more than 8 words, writing nothing, and files one of 8 words whose lone dash and bracket are not words. Pinned by `W-472-b1` "new refuses a 9-word title" and pinned by `W-472-b1` "newItem refuses a 9-word title" and pinned by `W-472-b1` "retro refuses a 9-word fix title" and pinned by `W-472-b1` "an 8-word title with a lone dash and bracket is filed" — limit: a record written by hand is not refused (check advises on it while it is not done); `retro`'s fix opera are filed through `newItem`, reused.
