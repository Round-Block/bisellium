## Intent

A signed brief and its opus record can drift apart unnoticed: the producer amends the record's title (`amend --title`) after the architect signed, the brief keeps the old scope, and nothing says so. W-183 is the case the Patron named: signed at round 9 (2026-10-11), its title amended four times since, each adding or trimming scope. W-179 drifted the same way: signed at round 3 (2026-10-11), retitled on 2026-10-15, and not re-signed until round 5 (2026-10-19).

After this opus, `bisellium check` advises `brief.stale` at `opera/<id>.md` when an open opus (greenlit, building, verifying, review) had its title amended after its newest architect signature, and the brief does not name the current title. The architect clears it by revising the brief and signing it again.

**What the signature pins.** No new field. A spec signature is `ci/<id>-spec-<n>.log` with `# phase: spec`, `# opus: <id>`, `# sella:` the design magister and `# outcome: passed`, and verdict stamps it `# at:`. `amend --title` appends an `amendments` entry `{ at, field: title, superseded: <the title before> }`. Together they give the title the brief was signed under: the `superseded` of the first title amendment after the signature, or the current title when no amendment followed. The rule does not use `# ticket:`'s `head` (the record's title at the signing commit). That would need a git read at a commit; logs older than tickets carry no ticket; and a title amended in the main checkout before signing, but not yet committed, would read as drift. The amendment log is the record's own history, and front matter is never edited by hand (CLAUDE.md).

**What "Files owned change" adds: nothing, so it is narrowed out.** Files owned lives in the brief, not the record. Editing it after a signature changes the brief's blob, and next's spec gate already refuses a brief that its newest signature's `# brief: briefs/<id>.md blob:<sha>` does not name while the trunk record is greenlit: the reviewed reading (`reviewedSpec`, W-162) and, with no reviewer, the bound reading before branch (`signedSpec`, W-293). Once the trunk record is past ready, next no longer reads the pin; a brief edit then rides the opus's own diff, which the build review reads. That is a stated limit, not this rule's: checking the blob here would copy next's pin reader (`blobOf`, not exported, in `next.ts`, W-299's lane), the duplicated source of truth an open lesson class names (design×indirection-duplicates-source-of-truth). The follow-on that adds next's hold can export it (Defaults 3). The probe agrees: on master 7ee93389, no brief of the 91 records with a spec log differs from its newest signature's pin (`probe.out`: 46 done briefs match, 26 done logs predate the pin (W-162), and every open or halted brief matches). The record's title should be amended to the narrowed scope (Defaults 1). This brief names that title, so the amendment is no finding:

Record title: `brief.stale (advise): the officina check marks a signed brief stale when its open opus record's title is amended after the architect's newest signature and the brief does not name the new title, so a brief cannot drift from its record unnoticed (W-183, W-179 drifted); a Files owned edit is the brief's own and its blob pin already voids the signature; filed at the Patron's request 2026-10-18`

**A retitle to the brief's own words is not drift.** Splitting or re-scoping an opus at spec ends with the producer amending its title to the brief's record-title line, minutes after the signature: W-297 (signed 23:38:20, retitled 23:39:44) and W-313 (signed 23:44:59, retitled 23:51:12). Both briefs hold the new title word for word (`probe-detail.out`). So the rule finds nothing when the brief names the current title, with whitespace runs read as one space. A title amended away and back again is no finding either.

**Level: advise, in check only; next does not hold on it in this opus.** On master today the rule flags five greenlit opera (W-169, W-183, W-208, W-229, W-276; `census.out`). Each needs an architect re-signature and the spec review that follows it. As a block, those five would fail the officina check on master, which CI's `officina` job runs on every lane, until all five were re-signed. As an advisory, every check run lists them and nothing else stops. next's spec gate is the natural place to hold (the branch step on a stale brief), but `packages/cli/src/next.ts` belongs to W-299 part 1, which is building in the other lane. The hold is a follow-on (Defaults 3).

**States judged: greenlit, building, verifying, review.** Done, halted and backlog are history. Without the state filter the probe flags four done opera (W-132, W-206, W-297, W-313); with it, none. The rule judges the tree being checked. On master that is a greenlit opus whose brief and logs are committed (a spec commit) or sit uncommitted in the main checkout before branch. After branch, the opus branch carries the brief and logs from its first commit, so check run on the branch judges them against the branch's copy of the record.

Promise stale-title: `check` advises `brief.stale` at `opera/<id>.md`, once per opus, when the record's state is greenlit, building, verifying or review, its newest architect signature carries an ISO `# at:`, a title amendment's `at` is later than that, the first such amendment's `superseded` differs from the current title, and `briefs/<id>.md` does not hold the current title (whitespace runs read as one space) — limit: it reads the amendment log, so a title edited by hand, with no amendment entry, is not seen; times are compared as the verbs stamped them; a newest signature with no readable `# at:` hides older ones (no finding); it judges only the tree being checked, so a title amended on master after branch is seen on the branch once the branch takes master.

Promise history-quiet: a done, halted or backlog opus is never flagged, and neither is an opus with no architect signature or an officina with no design collegium — limit: a halted opus that resumes to greenlit is judged again from its newest signature.

Promise advise-only: `brief.stale` is advise level, so no officina check's exit code changes, and `checkBriefStale` never throws: an unlistable `ci/` or `opera/`, an unreadable or unparseable record, and an unreadable log or brief each read as absent — limit: `next`, `branch` and `ready` do not run `checkStudio`, so none of them holds on it; it shows wherever `checkStudio` runs (`check`, `tick`'s health file, the console's check view).

Threat model: mistakes, not adversaries (D-045). Every file the rule reads is the officina's own record. Each read goes through the containment helper (`readContainedRegularFile`), so a path that escapes the officina is never read.
