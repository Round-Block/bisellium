## Intent

Today every greenlight is the Patron's word. `greenlight <opus>` takes any caller as the Patron: `main.ts` sets the patron role for it, and the verb writes a Patron timeline line. W-289 (greenlit, not built) is to refuse a greenlight the Patron did not run. The Patron ruled on 2026-10-24 and 2026-10-25 that two kinds of opus need no Patron greenlight, because their scope is already decided, and that there is no cap on them:

- A D-061 §4 remainder. A spec review that fails twice ends at a split: `next` names `new --parent <id>` for the remainder (`next.ts:1360`), and the parent's scope was the Patron's greenlight.
- A D-076 D cause fix. When an S1 class recurs, the cause fix is filed greenlit. W-381 made `next` hold a failed review on a class seen before until such an opus is filed (`next.ts:1483`).

This opus adds `--auto` to the verb. The question is how the verb can tell those two kinds from the records, without trusting a field the producer types alone. The records give one answer for each kind:

- A remainder. The opus's `parent:` names a record whose state is greenlit, building, verifying, review or done. The parent's spec logs under `ci/` (`<parent>-spec-<n>.log`, in round order) hold two failed reviews by the seat `bisellium.yml#spec_reviewer` names, with no passed review between. That is the split `next` reads at spec (`failedSpecReviews`, `next.ts:377`). The producer writes `parent:`, but the failed reviews are the reviewer's verdicts, written by `verdict`. So a free `parent:` admits nothing unless the parent really was split. Every spec log of the parent must read, so a log the verb cannot judge never hides a passed review between two failed ones.
- A cause fix. A class its title names, a whole token of the form `<area>×<slug>`, is the `[class:]` tag (`CLASS_TAG`, `verdict.ts:121`) of a standing blocker marked `[severity: S1]` in the failed review or spec logs of two or more other opera. That count is the census. The title only looks a class up, and the census decides, so a title naming a class no reviewer recorded twice admits nothing. A cause fix carries no `parent:`: an opus that carries one is read only as a remainder, and a cause fix that carries one is refused, whatever the parent (revision 4's cut; W-417 is to check that parent).

Free text may refuse, but never admits. The title, which is free text, is also screened for what stays the Patron's, and a match refuses. What the records hold but the verb cannot read or judge refuses: a record field in a form outside its domain, a parent record or parent spec log that cannot be read, a git failure reading CODEOWNERS, and a cause fix's parent, which this opus does not check. What reads and does not qualify is not that kind.

Each admission rests on a grant the Patron makes once: `bisellium.yml#auto_greenlit_by` names a decision by the Patron (`patronDecisionProblem`, reused). With no setting, `--auto` refuses. The decision is drafted as `D-114-draft.md`. It and the setting line are code-owned, so the Patron lands them (Defaults 5). Until then `--auto` refuses on the real officina.

On the real officina at master a34e02bc, with the setting given in memory and each record judged as if in backlog (`r5-real-probe.out`), 18 of 417 records qualify. Four are remainders: W-264, W-379, W-402 and W-417, which the Patron has greenlit already and which reads as this opus's own remainder (its spec reviews `ci/W-403-spec-2.log` and `ci/W-403-spec-4.log` failed). Fourteen are cause fixes, among them W-185, W-376, W-392 to W-395 and W-414. The screen refuses W-396 (a remainder whose title names `.github`) and W-383 (a cause fix whose title names "security"). The cut refuses W-381, which revision 3 admitted: its parent W-376 was reviewed once, so it is no remainder, and its title names `tests×ruled-case-uncovered`, which 7 opera's failed reviews carry, but it carries that parent. No real record meets another revision 3 refusal: every record's kind is native and its title a string, every parent's spec logs read with singular headers (`r3-enumerate.out`), and git reads the repository's CODEOWNERS.

What stays the Patron's (behaviour 2), refused before either kind is read:

- `kind: ui`, or a parent of `kind: ui` (D-091).
- A `value` that is not 1, 2 or 3 (D-061 §6's 3 points), or no value at all.
- A title naming, caseless as a whole token, `security`, `sandbox`, `architecture`, `branch protection`, `branch-protection`, `codeowners` or `code-owned`. Also the last segment of any plain pattern in `.github/CODEOWNERS` at the repository's HEAD (`.github`, `leges`, `ADOPTION.md` and so on), and a class of area `security`.
- A record the screen cannot read (behaviour 2): a kind outside the native kinds, a title `titleProblem` refuses, a `parent:` that is not a non-empty string, a parent record that exists but cannot be read or whose kind is missing or not native, and any git result on CODEOWNERS other than a confirmed non-repository or a HEAD whose tree has no such file.

Also the Patron's (behaviour 2), refused once both kinds are read: a cause fix that carries a `parent:` and is not that parent's remainder, whatever the parent (valid, absent, in a mistyped state). This is revision 4's cut under D-061 §4: spec round 4 found that the cause-fix path read a mistyped parent as "not a remainder". Checking that parent is W-417's.

The paths a spec will touch are not known at greenlight. So a spec that later sizes an auto-greenlit opus over 3 points, or that touches security, the sandbox or branch protection, still waits for the Patron before branch (production lex §7). A code-owned path still waits for the Patron's code-owner review at the pull request.

W-289's refusal is narrowed to match. It is not built (`writes.ts:791` on master refuses no caller), so this opus narrows it in its scope, not its code. D-114 §5 states that `--auto` is not the Patron's greenlight. The producer amends W-289's title through `amend` to say its refusal leaves `greenlight --auto` alone (Defaults 4).

An auto greenlight writes `state: greenlit`, `greenlit_by: <the decision>` and `greenlit_basis: <why>`, as the retro's high greenlight writes `greenlit_by` (`retro.ts:1042`). It emits `workflow.greenlight` granted, writes no Patron timeline line, and prints `<id>: greenlit (auto, <decision>: <why>)`. A refusal prints one line, `<id>: greenlight --auto refused: <why>`, exits 2 and writes nothing.

Threat model: mistakes, not adversaries (D-045). A producer who writes a false failed verdict could fake a split or a recurrence. W-399 (a record verb refuses a write whose sella is not its ticket's actor) is the fix for that.
