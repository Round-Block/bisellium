## Interfaces

**`amend <opus> --collegium <id>`.** `--collegium` is valued and joins the "at least one field" set. When master's earlier steps let the call reach them (an earlier refusal exits 2, an earlier throw exits 1, both before any write), it is refused with exit 2, before any write or event, in two cases:
- `<id>` fails check.ts's `ID_RE` (a letter or digit, then letters, digits, `.`, `_` or `-`) or contains `..`, or `declaredCollegiumIds(manifest)` does not list it. An empty value fails the rule;
- `<id>` equals the record's current `collegium` ("nothing to amend").

When the rest of `amend` admits the call, it sets `collegium` and appends `{at, sella, field: collegium, reason, superseded: <old or "">}` after any other field in the same call, and then emits one `workflow.item_amended` with `field: collegium`. The record is written first, with no rollback: a throw during or after that write exits 1 without the event (Promise collegium-move's limit).
