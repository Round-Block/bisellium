## Intent

The first real `bisellium land` (PR #1062, 2026-11-06) held after the Patron approved it. `land` waits for GitHub's `reviewDecision` to read `APPROVED`, and master's ruleset sets `required_approving_review_count` to 0 with `require_code_owner_review` true, so that field reads empty on an approved PR (`probe.out`: #1057, #1059, #1062, #1063; `survey-read.out`: every one of 71 code-owned PRs). W-509's rows ran on a stub `gh` that said `APPROVED`. Each rerun also requested the Patron's review again and sent the guard notice again: on #1062 the second request came at 14:39:21, after their approval at 14:37:14, and they approved again. The Patron: queuing should be automatic after approval.

After this opus: `land` opens the PR, asks the Patron once, and arms the queue. Their approval alone sends it in. A rerun is needed only to bring the merge to the local master, and the path that already works after a manual merge stays as it is.

**Decided: the approval is read from the reviews, on the current head.** One read, `gh pr view <n> --json headRefOid,reviews,reviewRequests,autoMergeRequest`. For each owner the CODEOWNERS screen names, the owner's latest opinion (`APPROVED`, `CHANGES_REQUESTED` or `DISMISSED`, by `submittedAt`; a comment is no opinion) must be an `APPROVED` whose `commit.oid` is the PR's head. Not `latestReviews`, whose `commit.oid` `gh` leaves empty (`probe.out`), so it cannot tell a head. The state alone is not enough either: #923 still lists an `APPROVED` on an old commit, beside the later `DISMISSED` (`real-923.json`), so "any approval" would read a dismissed PR as approved. Requiring the head does not lean on `dismiss_stale_reviews_on_push`.

**Decided: this read is not the gate.** It decides only what `land` does next: ask or not, stay for the merge or stop. GitHub's ruleset is what holds the merge. A wrong "approved" reaches GitHub's refusal; a wrong "not approved" leaves the PR armed, and GitHub still takes it in on the real approval.

**Decided: arm at once, with the gate's own call.** `armQueue` makes the same call as `gate`, `gh pr merge --squash --auto --match-head-commit <head>`, after the same three reads that can stop it (the queue is on, no conflict, no open code-scanning alert). It does not wait on checks, as `gate` does, because the point is that `land` can exit: GitHub holds an armed PR until its required checks pass. The one real case of an arm with a check still running is thin: #1063, armed by the Patron at 14:59:21, with `gates` finishing at 14:59:22 and code scanning at 14:59:55, entered at 15:00:10 (`probe-checks.out`); named step 1 meets it properly. It holds no background slot for the hours a Patron may take (#1007: 149 minutes). The other way, falling through to `gate`, needs a live process for the check wait, and that process's end at a timeout would leave a PR not armed.

**Decided: ask once.** `land` asks (the notice and the review request) only when no owner has a pending request and no owner has left any review on this head. So a rerun does not ask again, a comment or a change request on this head does not ask again, a head the owner has not seen does, and an approved PR never does. An unreadable read asks nothing and arms nothing: it may be an approval, and asking again is the bug this opus exists to end.

**The gate: what is proved, and what is not yet.** D-187 item 2 requires proof on a real PR that an armed, unapproved PR stays out of the queue, and that an approval on an old head does not count. No PR was created or armed for this spec.

- *Armed and unapproved: proved by GitHub's own record, read-only.* `next`'s `gate` has armed code-owned PRs before their approval all along. In `survey-read.out` (300 PRs, #773 to #1063, under this same ruleset, last changed 2026-10-13), 37 code-owned PRs were armed before the owner's first review, and each entered the queue after it, 3 to 146 seconds later. No code-owned PR merged with no owner review. The long waits rule out a race: #1007 was armed at 00:06:38, all required checks passed by 00:07:14, approved at 02:35:40, entered at 02:36:36; #1046 armed 02:29:02, approved 04:13:12, entered 04:13:55; #1015 armed 12:36:52, approved 14:01:47, entered 14:03:56 (`probe-checks.out`). So arming does not let an unapproved PR in, and this spec does not stop on that ground. Two PRs did enter the queue before any review, #978 and #1015; each of those entries came before the PR's first code-owned commit (`probe-queued.out`: D-158 at 16:45:05, D-171 at 12:36:35), and neither entered again until approved.
- *An approval on an old head, armed: not yet proved.* The record shows the dismissal firing on a push (#923 at 09:29:07, #1048 at 08:24:00), and neither PR re-entered before its new approval, but neither was armed in that gap, so it does not meet D-187's words. Named step 2 proves it, and needs the Patron. `land`'s own read is proved on the stub only.
- *A merge of master into the head keeps the approval.* #923's push at 09:05:57 was a merge of master (`git cat-file`, two parents); GitHub did not dismiss, reads the review on the new commit, and let it enter at 09:07:19. Its diff against the base did not change, so this is not an old-head approval, and `land`'s read follows GitHub. A land head does not take such a merge.
