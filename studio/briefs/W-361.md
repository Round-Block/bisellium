## Intent

D-100 selects candidate A from the W-361 research: Dracula's `#282A36` surface and stepped neutral wash/rule, high-contrast pale text, semantic colours and the darker drawer shadow. D-101 A amends its three semantic accents to Dracula's official Orange `#FFB86C`, Green `#50FA7B` and Red `#FF5555`; every other D-100 value stands. One exact palette replaces the light set before W-360 recaptures W-350's screenshots. There is no toggle, stored preference, alternate theme or JavaScript preference path.

The contract is executable rather than a copied contrast report. `W-361-b1` parses the values from `tokens.css`, computes WCAG 2.2 relative luminance and contrast for every ruled foreground/background pair, and requires at least 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for UI-component colour. This includes the amber Inbox count on the selected link's `--rule` fill and on the actual hovered-link fill. It also pins DIRECTION.md section 3 to the same values and to the D-101 wording. `--rule` remains a decorative hairline at 1.56:1 against surface and is not admitted as a component's sole state cue.

The producer's fixture census found existing rows that must flip with the palette. W-024 behaviour 1's top-level exact-colour checks migrate all nine old token expectations to D-100 as amended by D-101, W-341-b6 drops `bad` only from its text loop under D-100 B, and W-087 behaviour 4 migrates `--verdigris` plus the close button's computed outline from `#0b6e5f` / `rgb(11, 110, 95)` to `#3fb9a3` / `rgb(63, 185, 163)`. The migrations occur with the corresponding behaviour implementation. They are fixture migrations, not a second palette or rendering exceptions.

D-100 B says `bad` marks gates only and never colours text. The current source also spends it on `.seats__confirm-error`, `.board__card--bad` and Officina's high-burn pie. `W-361-b2` makes `.gate-ladder__mark--bad-diagonal` the only `--bad` consumer and keeps its diagonal shape. Confirmation error copy moves to `--ink`: it is actionable text, so the strongest text contrast is preferable to a weaker decorative status colour. A blocked Board card retains its 2px left-rule shape but uses `--ink`. Also set `.board-drawer__gate-evidence`, including its visited state, to `color: var(--verdigris)`; retain its underline.

Officina's budget graphic becomes `--ink` under allowance and `--amber` at or over allowance, matching the existing burn-bar vocabulary: over-budget work waits on a human response, while `bad` stays gate-only. Its non-colour cue is the adjacent posture word plus the exact numeric `burn / allowance tokens this period` reason. The graphic remains the existing component; this opus does not redesign the Officina layout.

The round-7 control sweep makes every current button, select, input and anchor text treatment explicit on the dark surface. It adds or changes `.board__strip-item` to ink, `.inbox__input-ratio::placeholder` to undimmed `ink-3`, `.seats__row select option` to ink on surface, `.board__project-select option` to `ink-2` on surface, and `.officina__row--link` from inherited colour to ink. There is no current textarea selector. The other current interactive selectors already declare their token and are enumerated by W-361-b2 so another omission cannot be closed one element at a time. D-101 B additionally makes `.sidebar__link--active` persistently underlined with `text-decoration-color: var(--ink)` over its `--rule` fill; W-361-b2 pins that source distinction from `.sidebar__link:hover`, and the owned desktop and phone Board rows pin the rendered current-page mark where they reach it.

Threat model: palette drift and accidental semantic-colour misuse, not adversaries (D-045).

`W-361-b1` obtains six-digit hex values from the `:root` block. For each RGB channel it divides the byte by 255, applies `c / 12.92` at or below 0.04045 and `((c + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4` otherwise, then uses `0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B`. Contrast is `(lighter + 0.05) / (darker + 0.05)`. It computes, rather than copies, all 22 assertions: `ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`, `verdigris`, `amber` and `ok` at 4.5:1 on both surface and wash; the amber Inbox count at 4.5:1 on `rule`, which is both the selected fill and the current hover fill; `verdigris`, `amber`, `ok` and `bad` at 3:1 on both surface and wash; and the ink current-page underline at 3:1 on its rule fill.
