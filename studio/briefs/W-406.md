## Intent

W-350’s frozen, loaded 1280×900 and 390×900 baselines exposed three layout defects before the Patron approved those images. At 1280, the Board’s 288px phase columns plus gaps and gutters exceed the main pane, the fourth phase is clipped, and nothing painted says that the row continues. DIRECTION keeps those 288px columns rather than shrinking them. The round-1 implementation styled Chromium’s native scrollbar pseudo-elements, but Playwright’s installed headless Chromium launch adds `--hide-scrollbars`; neither `apps/web/playwright.config.ts` nor `apps/web/playwright.serve.config.ts` overrides that default. The built capture therefore cannot paint that native scrollbar however its pseudo-elements are styled. W-406 retains the one native horizontal scroll container and adds a synchronized, always-painted in-page track and thumb immediately below it. The cue uses square neutral ruled tokens, needs no capture-runner change, and proves both continuation and terminal reach at 1280 and 390.

The Board currently stacks its visible connection/refresh line immediately above the colophon, making both read as footers, while the colophon begins flush at the main-pane edge. DIRECTION.md §4.5 selects the colophon as the only footer, but connection state and the last successful refresh remain sighted operating information. W-406 moves `.board__liveness` into the existing Board header, preserves its successful-refresh timestamp across unrelated renders and disconnects, retains the shell’s distinct polite live/stale announcements, wraps the phone header without horizontal overflow, and insets the one colophon to the title’s 24px gutter.

Officina’s today rule is vertically absolute but has no horizontal inset. Its static horizontal position shares the centre of the final 12px day column with the weekday glyph, so the rule crosses the last letter at both widths. Pinning the unchanged full-height 1px rule to the column’s trailing edge separates their rendered rectangles.

The Patron’s 2026-10-25 red, amber and Inbox-pane rulings in PR #758 are respected: this change adds no status colour, changes no amber or bad meaning, and does not touch Inbox layout. Threat model: layout regression, lost operating information and duplicate footer presentation, not adversaries (D-045).

Thumb width is `trackWidth × clientWidth / scrollWidth`; thumb offset is `(trackWidth − thumbWidth) × scrollLeft / (scrollWidth − clientWidth)`, with finite zero-range guards and values clamped to the track. The thumb captures its primary pointer on press. While captured, horizontal pointer delta maps to `scrollLeft` by the inverse ratio `delta × maxScroll / availableTrack`, clamped to `[0, maxScroll]`. A primary press on uncovered track jumps the thumb’s centre to the press coordinate, clamped to the track, then applies the same inverse ratio. Secondary buttons and non-primary pointers return before capture or scroll mutation. Those handlers write only `columns.scrollLeft`; the existing container scroll listener remains the one path that moves the rendered thumb. Cleanup removes every listener and observer. Drawer close restoration, initial phone positioning, strip-button scrolling and card focus movement update the cue through that same scroll listener.
