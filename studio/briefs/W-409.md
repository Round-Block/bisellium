## Intent

The Board currently makes a pending declared human gate a Needs-you membership and duplicates that opus into its lifecycle column, but it ignores linked open Inbox decisions, does not treat lifecycle backlog as the Board's greenlight ask, and gives every copy only the same amber rule. The shell separately displays the number of petition rows. The result is incomplete membership and an ask total rather than a work-item total. Current source evidence is `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-409-spec/apps/web/src/lib/board.ts`, `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-409-spec/apps/server/src/store.ts`, and `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-409-spec/apps/web/src/App.tsx`.

Extend those existing paths. `GET /api/inbox` keeps its response shape and widens its existing `opera` membership to include lifecycle states whose served phase is `backlog`; this is the greenlight ask. The Board joins that response to `/api/opera` by opus id. A backlog phase, one or many open petitiones, or a pending human gate puts the matching opus once in Needs you while the same opus stays once in its lifecycle phase. No matching `/api/opera` entry means no invented Board card.

Under D-135, the one shared Inbox-badge total is derived only from open `petitiones`, not from Inbox `opera`: linked questions count once per opus, while each open question with no opus link counts once under its own petitio id. Both the shell's Inbox nav badge and Inbox refresh report use it. A linked petitio naming an opus absent from the Board still counts once in the badge because it remains an open Inbox question, but it makes no ghost Board card. Greenlight asks and human gates remain Board Needs-you membership and add nothing to the Inbox badge. Ruling evidence is `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/records-1029j/studio/decisions/D-135.md` (landing by PR #865).

Every waiting card copy gets one compact neutral text line naming only the ask kind: `Greenlight`, `Inbox decision`, `N Inbox decisions`, or `Human gate`. Greenlight and Inbox may appear together, separated by a comma rather than DIRECTION's rejected middle-dot metadata string. The line appears on both the Needs-you and lifecycle copies so the phase column does not conceal why its amber rule is present. It adds no action, next actor, age, reason or evidence: W-410, W-412 and the existing drawer own those. The W-406 scroll cue/liveness and D-116 screen, W-415 shell, W-407 Inbox screens and W-442 Officina today rule remain intact.
