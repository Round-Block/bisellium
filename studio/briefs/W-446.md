## Intent

**The capture.** `capture.sh` ran the two files together under the served config, exactly as W-442's build did (`npx playwright test --config=apps/web/playwright.serve.config.ts apps/web/tests-serve/w406.spec.ts apps/web/tests-serve/w442.spec.ts` from the repository root, four tests on four workers, the bundle rebuilt by the global setup each run), 30 times on master be5f8108 on this host, beside the other lanes' jobs (one-minute load 5.9 to 16.1). `W-406-b1` failed 3 of them (runs 3, 5 and 11), every time at the same assertion with the same number, the number W-442's build recorded: "1280: track press jumps to its centred proportional offset" (`capture.out`), `expect(received).toBeLessThanOrEqual(expected)`, Expected `<= 1`, Received `1089.9951491632307`, after its 5 s poll. A failing `W-406-b1` took 6.5 to 6.6 s (its 5 s poll), a passing one 2.6 to 6.1 s. No other row failed, and 390 never failed. The three failures came in the first twelve runs, at load 6.0 to 7.9; none came in the eighteen runs at load 6.1 to 16.1 after them, so the rate is the host's timing, not a constant.

**The cause.** Received is `|scrollLeft − expected|`, and 1089.995 is the expected jump itself (at 1280 the track is 1012 px, the thumb 496.6 px and the scroll range 1100 px, so a press three quarters along jumps to (759 − 248.3) × 1100 / 515.4 ≈ 1090 px, `probe.out`): the press left `scrollLeft` at 0. The row's last steps, at each width, are: drag the thumb to the right terminal; set the columns' `scrollLeft` to 0; poll until `scrollLeft` reads 0; click the track at three quarters of its width. The painted thumb does not move when `scrollLeft` is set: `Board.tsx` moves it from the columns' `scroll` event (`updateScrollCue`), and the browser dispatches that event with its next frame. The poll reads 0 at once, so the click can arrive before that frame, while the thumb is still painted at the right terminal. At 1280 the thumb is 49% of the track (496.6 of 1012 px), so at the right terminal it spans 759 px to the track's end and covers the press point, 759 px along: the press hit-tests the thumb, `onThumbPointerDown` captures it and stops the event before the track's handler, the click has no movement, and `scrollLeft` stays 0. At 390 the thumb is 14% of the track (49.4 of 358 px) and never covers that point, so 390 cannot fail this way. Whether the click beats the frame depends on how fast the test's own round trips run against the renderer's frame on that host at that moment, which is why the row passes most runs, failed paired and passed in the full served suite. The instrumented copy of master's row, run beside `w442.spec.ts` 15 times (`probe.out`), records each press's hit target, the thumb's painted offset and `scrollLeft`: in the 14 passing runs the 1280 track press hit `board__scroll-cue` with the thumb's offset at 0px; in the one failing run (probe-15, the same assertion and the same Received) it hit `board__scroll-cue-thumb` with the offset still 515.37px, the right terminal, and `scrollLeft` already 0.

**The cause is in the row, not the Board.** Hit-testing follows the paint, so a person who presses where the thumb is painted grabs it; that is the cue working. `W-406-b1`'s own Input domain (row 3 of `studio/briefs/W-406.md`) defines the track press as "a primary button-0 press on uncovered track", and the row pressed covered track. So the fix waits for the cue's whole state, not half of it: before each press from the left terminal the row waits until the columns read `scrollLeft` 0 and the painted thumb stands at the track's left edge. Neither a retry nor a longer timeout: the press is made only once the state it presumes holds, the state `W-406`'s Interfaces names ("the existing container scroll listener remains the one path that moves the rendered thumb", and the row "presses uncovered track at three-quarters width"). Nothing in `Board.tsx`, which W-409 and W-451 own, changes.

**Forcing the race, deterministically.** A new row, `W-446-b1`, does not wait for the host's timing. After putting the columns and the painted thumb at the right terminal, it installs a fixture that holds the cue's next `scroll` update for 30 animation frames (a capture listener on the columns that stops that one event and dispatches it again 30 frames later, with no timer), the late frame; then it runs the shared left-terminal step and presses the track at three quarters, as `W-406-b1` does. On master's sequence the press lands on the held thumb and fails every run at "1280: track press jumps to its centred proportional offset" (`red-b1.out` and two repeats); with the wait it passes every run (`proto-b1-stage1.out` and two repeats).

**What the capture rules out.** Test order and shared server state: each row starts its own `bisellium serve` on its own port over its own temp copy of `examples/sample-studio` (`startServed`), and the failing runs ran the same four rows as the passing ones. Viewport: the row sets 1280×900 itself, and the cause needs the 1280 thumb's width. A scroll-snap or layout race: the row turns snapping off, the failing value is exactly the unmoved `scrollLeft`, and the probe shows the hit target. A longer timeout: the failing poll waited its full 5 s on a `scrollLeft` that never moved; no wait after the press could pass it.

## Interfaces

The prototype is the series `series.mbox` over master be5f8108, built by `stages.sh`: `test(W-446): row b1, red` adds `board-cue.ts` with master's sequence (`proto/board-cue.red.ts`: set `scrollLeft` 0, poll until it reads 0) and `w446.spec.ts`; `feat(W-446): behaviour 1` replaces `board-cue.ts` with the waiting step (`proto/board-cue.ts`) and edits `w406.spec.ts` by `apply-w406.py`.

**`scrollBoardToLeftTerminal(columns: Locator, cue: Locator, thumb: Locator, gesture: string): Promise<void>`** (`apps/web/tests-serve/board-cue.ts`). Sets the columns' `scrollLeft` to 0; polls until it reads 0 (message `<gesture> starts at the left terminal`, the message `W-406-b1` already uses); then polls until the thumb's bounding box starts within 1 px of the cue's (message `<gesture> starts with the painted thumb at the left terminal`). Each poll is `expect.poll` with Playwright's defaults (5 s). It does not change scroll snapping; the caller does.

**`W-406-b1`** (`apps/web/tests-serve/w406.spec.ts`). Before the drag it turns snapping off (`style.scrollSnapType = "none"`) and calls `scrollBoardToLeftTerminal(columns, cue, thumb, `${width}: drag`)`; before the track press it calls `scrollBoardToLeftTerminal(columns, cue, thumb, `${width}: track press`)`. Every assertion and message is unchanged; one is added per step.

**`W-446-b1`** (`apps/web/tests-serve/w446.spec.ts`, `W-446-b1 a Board track press after a scroll to the left terminal jumps to its centred offset when the cue repaints late`). At 1280×900 it starts a served instance, signs in at `#/board`, and asserts in order: the Board has more than one phase ("1280: the served Board has every phase"); the thumb is painted; with snapping off and `scrollLeft` at its maximum, the phases overflow ("1280: the phases overflow into a horizontal scroll range") and the painted thumb reaches the track's right edge within 1 px ("1280: the painted thumb starts at the right terminal"); the right-terminal thumb starts left of the press point, three quarters along the track ("1280: the right-terminal thumb covers the track press point"). It then installs the fixture (a `scroll` listener on the columns, capture phase, once: it stops the event's propagation, counts it in `window.w446Held`, and dispatches a new `scroll` event on the columns after `HOLD_FRAMES` = 30 `requestAnimationFrame` callbacks), calls `scrollBoardToLeftTerminal(columns, cue, thumb, "1280: track press")`, clicks the press point at the track's vertical centre, and asserts that `scrollLeft` reaches the centred proportional offset within 1 px ("1280: track press jumps to its centred proportional offset", `W-406-b1`'s own formula and message) and that the fixture held exactly one update ("1280: the fixture held the cue's scroll update once").
