### Input domain

A rejection fails closed: nothing is written, or `next` holds or orders the architect, never the build.

| Record | Valid domain | Rejected by |
|---|---|---|
| A spec review's `## Findings` | one section holding the exact line `No findings` or numbered `blocking`/`advisory` lines, each ending `check: <value>`; a blocker cites a non-blank `brief:<line>` | `findingsProblem`: any other body is refused, exit 2, nothing written (unchanged); then `reconcileFindings` records an uncited blocker advisory; `sessionedProblem` refuses a sessioned log that disagrees (unchanged) |
| A spec review's recorded outcome | `passed` or `failed`, agreeing with the standing blockers | `readSpecReview`: anything else holds `next` at `spec` (unchanged) |
| The logs counted for the cap | a failed spec review as `bisellium verdict` writes it, in a log named `<id>-spec-<n>.log` | `reviewedSpec`: a bad name holds (unchanged); `readSpecReview`: a log it does not accept as a `failed` review is not counted. Limit: the counter takes `readSpecReview`'s reading of each log; malformed or hand-edited history is out of scope, and W-252 closes it |
