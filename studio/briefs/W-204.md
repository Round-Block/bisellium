## Intent

Decree family: uncapped-review-loop

D-053 supersedes D-046 §2 and D-051. A failed build-review round is followed by the ordinary fix and review rungs, at any round number. The change deletes:
- `next`'s cap: the `round_rulings` read, the counted-failure test and its Patron hold;
- the review order's security-exemption sentence;
- `amend --round-ruling` and the reader only it and `next` used.

Historical records (D-043, D-048, D-051's grants, W-166's `round_rulings`) are inert data that every reader still accepts. Strict review-history, configuration, gate and tree reads are unchanged and still fail closed. Security findings stay blocking findings (D-044). The threat model is mistakes (D-045). Reused, unchanged code is judged by its result (D-050).

## Defaults for the Patron to veto

1. `--round-ruling` is removed from the grammar and refused as an unknown flag, with no tombstone message. On a veto, a `NOT_AMENDABLE` row citing D-053 refuses it by name before parsing, a data-only addition.
2. `BuildReviewRound.at` and `.securityOnly` stay, parsed and unread, so `parseBuildReviewLog` is unchanged. On a veto, both are deleted, with rows for that parser's reads.
3. `patronDecisionProblem` keeps its unused `grantLine` and `after` options, so its four live callers keep an unchanged reader. On a veto, the options are deleted, with rows for each caller.
4. `buildReviewKeys` keeps `patron`, so a worktree whose `patron` differs still holds. On a veto, `patron` is dropped, and W-167-b1's patron row flips to the unrelated-key result.
5. `next` prints no count of failed rounds and no warning, at any round. D-053's kill condition (ten failed rounds of one kind) is the producer's to notice. On a veto, an advisory line is added, never a hold.
