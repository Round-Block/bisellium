## Intent

Extract the console's current colours into one Dracula theme without changing any painted pixel. `apps/web/src/themes/dracula.css` owns every palette value and binds a complete set of roles named by meaning. `tokens.css` imports that one theme and keeps every old colour token as a compatibility alias, so existing consumers remain unchanged. Equal values remain separate roles when their meanings differ: selected surfaces and two border purposes; links and facts; product actions and focus; generic state indicators and W-427 severity; text selection and categories.

This is only the D-217 item 8 extraction. It does not correct current Dracula gaps. Purple headings, persistently underlined Classic Cyan links, Purple focus, border choices, exception rulings and consumer migration remain later children. In particular, `apps/web/src/components/w025.css` is byte-for-byte outside this change. W-503 may resume its Home work without a shared source file, and W-467 re-signs afterward against `--color-heading`, `--color-link`, `--color-fact`, `--color-category`, the three text roles, the surface/border/focus roles and the four severity roles.

The D-217 item 9 guards that belong here are: one complete exact Dracula role set; no authored colour literal outside `themes/*.css`; no `--dracula-*` palette consumption outside its theme; every compatibility alias points to its declared role; and browser-computed alias/role equality on every shipped screen and capture row. Cross-theme role parity, per-theme computed contrast, served axe per theme, colour-vision checks and compatibility-alias removal wait until a second theme or the later verification/migration children exist. Existing single-theme W-339, W-341, W-361 and W-366 rows remain active and are migrated to resolve the theme graph rather than weakened.
