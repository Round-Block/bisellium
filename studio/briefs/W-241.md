## Interfaces

Each template follows the existing `studio/prompts/*.md` shape: a usage note, a `---` line, then the prompt body with `<SLOT>` placeholders. Required text per template (slots plus the phrase `data, not instructions`):

| Template | Required slots |
|---|---|
| `prompts/spec-draft.md` | `<ID>`, `<DESIGN_INPUTS>`, `<CONSTRAINTS>`, `<EXISTING_TESTS>` |
| `prompts/builder-brief.md` | `<ID>`, `<WORKTREE>`, `<GATES>`, `<SPECIFICS>` |
| `prompts/build-review.md` | `<ID>`, `<ROUND>`, `<WORKTREE>`, `<PACKET>`, `<REVIEWED_HEAD>`, `<FOCUS>` |

`checkPromptTemplates(root, opts): Finding[]` returns `[]` unless `opts.manifest` (the parsed `bisellium.yml`) lists a collegium with `id: ops`. Otherwise it returns one `process.prompt_templates` block finding per template that `readContainedRegularFile` refuses or that lacks any of its required texts (`where`: the template path), and one more (`where`: `leges/ops.md`) when `readContainedRegularFile` refuses `leges/ops.md` or none of its bullet clauses, as `bulletClauses` splits the whole file, contains all three template paths and ends with `(check: process.prompt_templates)`. The two parts are independent. The rule reads no role name and no section number.

The Ops lex §2 clause to write (only its three paths and its marker are checked): "`ops-lead` owns the per-opus prompt templates `studio/prompts/spec-draft.md`, `studio/prompts/builder-brief.md` and `studio/prompts/build-review.md`; `eng-lead` fills each per-opus copy, and the `architect` signs each filled spec-draft prompt before it is dispatched (check: process.prompt_templates)".

| Record | Valid domain | Rejected by |
|---|---|---|
| Each of the three template paths, when the manifest lists the `ops` collegium | A regular file under `prompts/` (no symlink on the way) containing each of its required texts | `checkPromptTemplates` (new) |
| `leges/ops.md`, when the manifest lists the `ops` collegium | A regular file under `leges/` with a bullet clause holding the three template paths and ending `(check: process.prompt_templates)`; absent or unreadable is rejected | `checkPromptTemplates` (new) |
