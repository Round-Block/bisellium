## Intent

W-241 shipped as four parts; D-069 C kept part 1 here and moved the lead agents to W-294. Today the orchestrator writes each architect dispatch and each first builder brief by hand from two kept files outside the repository (`~/.bisellium-evidence/architect-dispatch-model.md`, `~/.bisellium-evidence/builder-brief-model.md`), and the production lex still points at older ones; only the round-1 build review prompt is checked in, read by next's review perform. W-258 (the builder's self-check) waits on a checked-in builder brief (D-082 D), and W-270 adds its lesson classes to the spec-draft prompt.

After this opus the three per-opus prompts live in `studio/prompts/` in the existing shape (a usage note, a `---` line, the body with `<SLOT>` placeholders): `spec-draft.md` and `builder-brief.md` (new) and `build-review.md` (unchanged). The Ops lex gives them to `ops-lead` in one §2 clause, and the production lex runbook names them. A new rule, `process.prompt_templates`, blocks `bisellium check` when the officina declares the `ops` collegium and a template is missing, is not a regular file, or lacks one of its slots or the phrase `data, not instructions`, or when no Ops lex clause names all three and ends with its marker.

Promise templates-present: in an officina whose manifest declares the `ops` collegium, `bisellium check` blocks, once per template path, unless that path is a regular file under `prompts/`, reached through no symlink, holding each of its required texts — limit: a required text is found as a literal substring anywhere in the file, the usage note above the `---` line included, so presence is checked and wording is not; an officina without the `ops` collegium is not checked; the path is admitted only as `readContainedRegularFile` (reused unchanged, D-076 E) admits it: no component a symlink, opened without following a link as a regular file whose real parent and real target stay under `prompts/`; every refusal of it, a thrown error included, comes back as a refusal and is a block.

Promise review-slots-agree: the build-review slots the rule requires are exactly the slots next's review perform fills (`BUILD_REVIEW_SLOTS`, `packages/cli/src/next.ts`), so a template the check accepts is one next fills: both find a slot only as the literal substring `<NAME>` anywhere in the text, so they agree on every text — limit: the two lists are separate code, held equal by row `W-241-b1` through the exported `fillSlots`, not shared; next.ts belongs to W-299 here and W-331 splits it (the row then imports `fillSlots` from where it lands), and a later opus may make next import the rule's table; `fillSlots` is reused unchanged.

Promise clause: `bisellium check` blocks on `leges/ops.md` unless one of its bullet clauses, as `bulletClauses` joins it, holds the three template paths and ends with `(check: process.prompt_templates)` — limit: a clause is exactly what `bulletClauses` (reused unchanged, D-076 E) returns, the forms Input domain row 2 lists, and every other form is a limit; only the three paths and the marker are read, anywhere in the file; the role names, the section and the wording are not checked; fences and HTML comments are not recognised, so a delimiter on the line after the clause joins it and blocks, and one a blank line separates leaves the clause to pass.

Threat model: mistakes, not adversaries (D-045): a template deleted or renamed in a cleanup, a slot dropped in an edit, the ownership clause lost in a lex rewrite.
