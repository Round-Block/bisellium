## Intent

The approved master drawer exposes two cause-level defects. First, the lifecycle track keeps the horizontal offset used to expose an opener in the wider closed Board after the drawer narrows that Board. At 1280 with the 220px expanded sidebar, the pushed workspace leaves a 520px Board beside the fixed 540px drawer; retaining W-004's 164px closed-layout offset puts earlier content beneath the shell edge without keeping W-004's own Awaiting review context at the pushed viewport inset. The correct target is therefore the opened lifecycle column, not a fixed zero or the closed-layout offset. Current source evidence is `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-499-spec/apps/web/src/screens/Board.tsx`; measured base geometry is `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-493-review/master-drawer/drawer-1280-expanded.png`.

Second, a legacy petitio with no authored `subject:` gets a capped subject derived from its first body paragraph. `drawerDetail()` then joins that derived label back to the complete body as `${subject}: ${body}`. Its final `Set` removes identical whole bullets but cannot remove repetition inside one joined string, producing the capture's `new…: Drag-and-drop…`. Current source evidence is `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-499-spec/apps/web/src/lib/board.ts`, the derivation contract is `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-499-spec/adapters/native/src/index.ts`, and the observed defect is `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-493-review/master-drawer/drawer-390.png`.

When the pushed drawer mounts for a lifecycle item, align that item's column with the lifecycle viewport's left inset, clamped to the track's available scroll range. Planned therefore stays contained when W-007 opens, W-004 keeps Awaiting review beside its drawer, and a terminal opener lands as far left as the track permits. Clip only the first 20px of the 24px leading gutter, then extend the inner card scroller's paint box 4px on all four sides with equal negative margin and padding: at an aligned target and at scroll zero, preceding-column fragments stay out of the leading inset while both clip ancestors preserve the focused card's complete 2px outline and 2px offset, including above the first card, with or without the drawer open. Column and card geometry, scroll targets, range and reachability do not change. At other offsets, preceding content may occupy the reserved 4px strip. An item with no lifecycle column keeps the current position. Preserve the latest pre-open lifecycle and waiting-strip snapshots and, when the drawer closes, restore each browser-reported value clamped to the closed layout's available range. User scroll while open and right-edge in-track clipping are limits. Phase order, widths, the one local scroller and terminal reachability remain those approved under D-115/D-116, D-119, D-157/D-158 and D-162.

At exactly 640 CSS px, the 540px drawer plus a 100px Board remnant cannot leave one readable lifecycle column. The Patron's 2026-11-02 ruling therefore gives an open drawer the full available content row beneath the navigation row, matching the existing 390 presentation. The Board stays mounted in a zero-width grid cell and becomes inert while open; Close remains visible and focused. Closing removes inert, returns the Board to the row, preserves the latest opener's selection and focus, and restores both saved scroll positions within their newly available ranges. At 1280 the existing fixed 540px drawer and pushed visible Board remain unchanged.

The Patron's 2026-11-03 screen look found that cross-source accumulation still repeats one wait in different words and that the banner repeats the question while routing only to the Inbox screen. Separate those jobs. With a petitio, the banner reads `Waiting on you — Go to Inbox`, links only `Go to Inbox` to `#/inbox`, and uses an ordinary underlined text link rather than the cyan evidence-link treatment. Without a petitio it reads `Waiting on you — no question recorded.` with no link. `Why it waits` chooses exactly one source group in priority order: every usable linked petitio body in source order, with subject fallback for a blank body and first-sender attribution; otherwise nonblank recorded `next`; otherwise descriptions of recorded outstanding human gates; otherwise the honest `No waiting reason recorded.` state. Trim outer whitespace and collapse exact duplicate text within the chosen group, keeping its first occurrence; never append a lower-priority group. The record's `next`, gate statuses, petitio subject and Inbox presentation remain available in their existing sections.
