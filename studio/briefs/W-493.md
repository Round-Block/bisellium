## Intent

Board cards currently render the six declared gates as unlabelled squares, including hollow squares on Planned cards. The item drawer currently puts `From the officina record` after waiting facts, reasons, evidence, the named gate list and events. Current source evidence is `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-493-spec/apps/web/src/screens/BoardView.tsx` and `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-493-spec/apps/web/src/screens/BoardDrawer.tsx`.

Replace each non-Planned card's squares with one short line: `cleared/total · next: name` while gates remain, `cleared/total · complete` when all declared gates are cleared, and `0/0 · no gates` when none are declared. Passed and waived gates are cleared. The next gate is the first declared gate whose status is not passed or waived. Planned cards, including lifecycle backlog cards which the Board places in Planned, render no progress line.

Move the whole existing record section directly below the drawer header. Its authored description, record facts and empty-body fallback stay together. Waiting facts, reasons, evidence, the named gate list and events retain their existing internal order and content after it. This intentionally flips the square and drawer-order contracts shipped around W-411 and used by W-487; D-157 identified both as later work, and W-493 now supplies that ruling.
