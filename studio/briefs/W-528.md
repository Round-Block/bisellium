## Intent

The `gates` job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` has no `timeout-minutes`, so a hung run holds it to GitHub's 360-minute default. W-527 found one: PR 1032's merge-group run 41801848233 printed its test summary, never exited, and was cancelled at 360 minutes, the largest single loss in 53 failed merge-group runs. `certify` has `timeout-minutes: 100` and `certificate` has 10; `gates`, `officina` and `web-e2e` have none (read on master e887cfcc). This opus gives `gates` a limit and nothing else.

**Decided: `timeout-minutes: 60` on the `gates` job, and on no step.** The number comes from measured passing gates jobs. W-527's report gives run wall-clock minutes, not job minutes, so the job times were read from GitHub: W-527's kept `jobs.json` files (54 gates jobs, 16 passing) plus the 60 newest successful merge-group runs and the 60 newest successful master pushes (`pull-gates.sh`, 2026-11-04 to 11-07). Across the 136 passing gates jobs, 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-07, the median is 20.4 minutes (24.7 across the 88 that ran the full suite; the other 48 are pull-request or record-only runs of 2 minutes or less) and the slowest is 31.8 (run 40113720669, 2026-10-24); the newest master pushes run 29 to 31 (30.9, run 42374215863, 2026-11-07) (`gates-times.out`, `job-times.out`). 60 is about 1.9 times the slowest, a margin of 28 minutes for suite growth and a slow runner, while a hang now costs 60 minutes, not 360. A tighter 45 leaves 13 minutes over the slowest when the newest runs already sit at 31, which a few weeks of new rows could use up and turn a passing run red.

**Gates sits under certify's 100, and both sit under the merge queue's 120.** `certify` runs a superset of gates' work in one job (two `npm ci`, a Playwright install and the whole mint, its tests among them; its slowest passing job is 32.4 minutes), so a gates limit at or over certify's would let the lighter job outlive the heavier. The ruleset's merge queue waits 120 minutes for a required check (`check_response_timeout_minutes: 120`, read live on 2026-11-07, `queue-rules.out`): with no limit the queue gave up on PR 1032 at 120 while the job ran on to 360; at 60, gates fails first and the runner is freed.

**Kept outside `CI_STEPS`.** `packages/commands/src/ci.ts`'s `CI_STEPS` is a list of `run:` commands, and `scripts/ci-workflow.test.mjs` compares it with the workflow's `run:` lines only; a job key is no step and has nothing to mirror. Locally, `bisellium ci` already bounds each step by `GATE_TIMEOUT_MS`, 30 minutes (`packages/pipeline/src/index.ts`), which is reused unchanged.

**Facts checked, against the brief.**
