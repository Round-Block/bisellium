## Interfaces

`buildContext(root, sella, {now, maxTokens?})` gains one section, `runbook`:
- **Source.** Count the lines of the sella's collegium lex that match `^## \d+\. Opus ladder$`. Zero such lines add no runbook section. Exactly one supplies the runbook: the bytes from that line up to the next `^## ` line (or EOF), without the newline in front of that next line. Two or more are ambiguous (below).
- **Placement.** It renders right after `## CLI usage` as `## Runbook` followed by `--- data: <lex path> §<n> ---`, the bytes, and `--- end ---`.
- **Truncation.** The loop never drops it: it removes other sections first, and the bundle may exceed `maxTokens` rather than cut the runbook.
- **Two or more matching lines.** `buildContext` returns `{text: "", tokens: 0, truncated: ["runbook ambiguous"]}`, and `bisellium context` prints `runbook ambiguous: <sella>` and exits 1.
- **The full lex** stays an ordinary droppable section. At budgets large enough to keep it, §9 appears twice; that is accepted.

This repo's SessionStart and PreCompact hooks (`.claude/settings.json`, unchanged by this part) run the bare installed `bisellium context --studio studio`. The sella is `$BISELLIUM_SELLA`, or `guest` when unset (main.ts line 194); `guest` and `producer` both sit in the production collegium.

Promise session-boots-with-runbook: a session whose sella is in the production collegium gets lex §9 in its boot context — limit: only once the Patron has updated the installed CLI (`~/projects/bisellium-guard`, today at 12a9041) to merged master. Until then the stale installation serves today's bundle without §9.

On `runbook ambiguous`, the bare `context` prints one stderr line and exits 1, so the session boots with no injected context. The `hook-event context` target that `hooks print` generates prints an empty bundle and exits 0 (`handleContext` in `packages/cli/src/hooks.ts`).

Promise builder-sections-unchanged: a sella whose collegium lex has no "Opus ladder" line gets no `## Runbook` section, and its other sections and truncation order are those of master — limit: shown by the existing `context.test.ts` rows staying green and b2's no-runbook assertion, not by a byte comparison with master's `context.ts`.
