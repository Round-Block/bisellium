## Intent

Four `ci --opus` runs failed their tests gate with exit 1 and a log in which no test fails. The diagnosis proves why the log is silent, and proves the cause is not visible from the logs:

- **The log drops stdout.** `localPipeline` (`packages/pipeline/src/index.ts`) writes `${stdout}${stderr}` cut to its last 200 lines, and `verify` prints the same 200-line tail on a failure. Every `not ok`, `FAIL` and `✖` line is on stdout; the suite's stderr is about 1,300 lines (the green run's `full.err`), so the tail is all stderr. ADOPTION.md already states the contract: stderr last, "a gate must not rely on where in that tail its own output lands".
- **The row-3 fixture leaks.** `scripts/no-vendor.test.mjs` behaviour 9 row 3 spawns a child runner with stderr inherited; its sentinel report (`vendor-sentinel: 1 vendor-spend violation(s) detected:` and `row3-violation`) is the row's fixture, yet it prints on every run, green too, and reads as a real violation in a failing log.
- **The cause is not in the logs.** W-443 failed about 10 s after that fixture's output and W-446 in or after the `next-*` group (concurrency 8, about 14 minutes); every candidate file passes alone, and a quiet full run passes.

**Where (a) is fixed, and why there.** In `scripts/test-reads.mjs`, the one command the tests gate of `ci --opus` runs (`TEST_READS` in `packages/commands/src/ci.ts`) and the command `bisellium ci`'s own loop runs for `npm test`. It already puts its guard line last on stderr for this very reason. It now passes `npm test`'s stdout through as it arrives while keeping its last 50 failing lines and its last 50 lines, and when `npm test` fails it ends stderr with both, under one header each: at most 102 lines, inside the 200-line tail of both the stored log and `verify`'s print. The pipeline seam, `verify`, the log's header lines and ADOPTION.md's contract are unchanged; a code-owned file is not touched. Not in `localPipeline`: the log body is the documented contract of every adopter's gate (ADOPTION.md, code-owned) and of certify's mint, judged at its own merge by master's runner (W-323). Not in `scripts/no-vendor.mjs`: its `runOnce` is synchronous and driven directly by its own 11 behaviours, and `spawnSync` cannot pass output through while it keeps it.

**(b)** Row 3's child gets a piped stderr, and the row asserts it is exactly the sentinel report, so the report is checked where it is made and no longer reaches the suite's stderr.

**(c) The cause is not fixed here.** My prototype runs did not reproduce it: the `next-*` group, run exactly as `test:suite` runs it (concurrency 8, TAP), passed 191 of 191 tests in 9 minutes on this host (`repro/next-1.exit`, from `repro/next-group.sh`), as did the diagnosis's quiet full run and its alone-runs of the 14 files after W-443's failure point. A fix now would be a guess, and this brief does not guess. With (a), the next failing run names its test, and the cause is fixed at that test in a follow-up opus (Defaults 2); no row is rerun as a remedy (lex §11).

## Interfaces

**`failingLine(line: string): boolean`** (exported from `scripts/test-reads.mjs`): true for a line that, after any leading whitespace, opens with `not ok <digit>`, `FAIL` and a whitespace character, `✖ ` (U+2716 and a space), `# fail <1-9>` or `ℹ fail <1-9>` (U+2139): the failing lines of Node's TAP reporter, its spec reporter and the house `PASS/FAIL` harnesses.

**`FAILING_MAX = 50`, `STDOUT_TAIL = 50`** (exported).

**`runTestReads(repo, log): Promise<number>`**: as before (W-335), `npm test` with the trace's two keys, then the guard only on exit 0, resolving the first non-zero status (1 for a signal or a spawn failure); `npm test`'s stdin and stderr are inherited and its stdout is piped, each chunk written to this process's stdout as it arrives. When `npm test`'s status is not 0, before resolving it prints on stderr `--- npm test failed (exit <status>): its last <k> of <n> failing lines on stdout ---`, the last `FAILING_MAX` stdout lines `failingLine` accepts (`<n>` counts them all), `--- npm test: its last <m> lines of stdout ---` and the last `STDOUT_TAIL` stdout lines, each line whole. A passing run prints nothing new. The main block exits with that status through `process.exitCode`.

**`scripts/no-vendor.test.mjs` behaviour 9 row 3**: the child `A` is spawned with `stdio: ["pipe", "pipe", "pipe"]`; its stderr is collected to its `close`, and a fourth row-3 check, `row 3 (W-466): A's own stderr, piped, is exactly its sentinel report of the violation`, passes when it equals `vendor-sentinel: 1 vendor-spend violation(s) detected:\n  row3-violation\n`, printing it as the check's detail.

Promise blocks: a failed `npm test` under `node scripts/test-reads.mjs` ends the tests gate's log with its failing lines in order and its last 50 stdout lines, after the suite's own stderr, however loud that stderr. Pinned by `W-466-b1` "the gate log names the failing row" and pinned by `W-466-b1` "the log ends with the failing lines in order and the last 50 stdout lines" and pinned by `W-466-b1` "the blocks follow the suite stderr" — limit: only `node scripts/test-reads.mjs` prints them (`bisellium ci`'s loop and the tests gate of `ci --opus`); a gate that runs plain `npm test` (a bare `verify`, certify's mint) and ci.yml's `npm test` step keep today's output, and a failure's detail beyond its failing line (the TAP diagnostic block, an uncaught stack on stderr) survives only where it falls in the last 50 stdout lines or the roughly 98 lines of the log's tail above the blocks.

Promise forms: the failing lines are TAP's `not ok <n>` and a non-zero `# fail`, the spec reporter's `✖` and a non-zero `ℹ fail`, and the house harness's `FAIL`, at any indent; a zero count, a glued word, a `PASS` row and an `ok` row are not. Pinned by `W-466-b1` "the log ends with the failing lines in order and the last 50 stdout lines" — limit: any other failure form (an uncaught exception, a custom message, a Playwright summary) is not a failing line, and a passing test that echoes a failing fixture's output on stdout is listed too (the green run's three `✖` lines and one `ℹ fail 1` are such echoes, `full.out` lines 5005-5018).

Promise cap: at most the last 50 failing lines are kept, and the header counts every failing line. Pinned by `W-466-b1` "the last 50 failing lines, counted out of all 121" and pinned by `W-466-b1` "an earlier failing line is not kept" — limit: an earlier failing line past the 50 is dropped; the chain stops at its first failing file, so the last lines are that file's.

Promise pass: a passing `npm test` passes its stdout through whole and prints nothing new; its stderr still ends with the guard (W-335). Pinned by `W-466-b1` "a passing suite passes" and pinned by `W-466-b1` "its stdout passes through whole" and pinned by `W-466-b1` "a passing suite repeats nothing" and pinned by `W-466-b1` "its stderr ends with the guard, as before" — limit: that stdout arrives as it is written, not at the end, is not pinned (only its whole content is), and a signal or a spawn failure of `npm test` resolves 1 as before, unpinned here.

Promise fixture: no-vendor.test.mjs behaviour 9 passes with row 3's sentinel report on its own pipe, asserted exactly, and none of it on its stderr. Pinned by `W-466-b2` "behaviour 9 passes" and pinned by `W-466-b2` "none of the row 3 sentinel report reaches the stderr of behaviour 9" and pinned by `W-466-b2` "behaviour 9 asserts the row 3 report on its own pipe, exactly" — limit: other rows' own stderr (behaviour 9 row 4's lock message is printed as a check detail on stdout) is not judged.
