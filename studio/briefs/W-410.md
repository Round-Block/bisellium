The owning agent is the existing opus `sella`. The next actor is the recorded `traditio.blocked_on`; a missing, blank or literal `none` value displays `Unassigned` rather than guessing. The wait starts at the oldest parseable instant among active ask sources: petition `opened`, a pending declared human gate's `at`, or the handoff `at` for a greenlight or as the human-gate fallback. The numeral shows whole seconds below one hour, whole hours below one day and whole days thereafter; a missing or invalid instant displays `—`, and clock skew floors at `0s`. This display-only model is pinned in `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-410-spec/apps/web/test/w410.test.ts`.

Reasons and evidence move off the compact card and into the existing drawer. For a waiting card the drawer displays, in source order, linked petition subject/body, a declared human gate only when its recorded result status is `pending`, and the recorded handoff next step, followed by gate evidence links already returned by the API. The opus body remains solely in `From the officina record`; a declared gate with no recorded result contributes no reason or waiting banner. Empty reasons and evidence state their absence. This is display only: W-412 owns reviewed-revision binding, gate-result/decision-record naming and stale-verdict refusal. W-410 does not certify freshness or equivalence. Evidence is `/home/edckt/agents/bisellium/.worktrees/W-410-spec/apps/web/src/screens/BoardDrawer.tsx` and `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-410-spec/shots/board-drawer-390.png`.
