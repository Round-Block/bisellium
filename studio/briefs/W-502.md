## Intent

`next W-413` took 70 s from the main checkout on 2026-11-04; other opera answered in under 2 s. The audit blamed loading 501 records per call, and the opus record blamed the branch and its logs. The profile says neither.

**Where the time goes.** All of it is W-316's silence read. A dispatch order with no step marker (`deriveNext(f).order` set, the marker read `none`) walks every regular file under `<repo>/.worktrees/<id>` and `<id>-*` and `~/.bisellium-evidence/<id>` and `<id>-*`, to find the newest. `~/.bisellium-evidence/W-413-spec` holds 5104374 entries outside `node_modules` and `.git`: 746 throwaway directories a prototype's scripts left behind (673 `stage*`, 31 `series*`, 22 `mbox-check.*`, 20 `proto*`), most of them a copy of the repository. No other opus comes near: W-451 is next at 70024 entries, W-209 at 28290, W-364 at 14611 (`census.out`, 196 opera, one over 100000). The same state, reproduced in a throwaway clone so that the running review's marker does not hide the read (`lab.sh`, W-413 at 729f7ce6: a build order with no marker), took 75.4 s, 59.7 s and 47.0 s; of the 59.66 s profiled, `silence` and `newestFile` are 57.34 s (96.1%): `readdirSync` 33.48 s, `lstatSync` 22.29 s (`prof/cpu-order-before-2.txt`). Everything else is 2.3 s, and half of that is `gh` (1.25 s, two calls). The cost follows the state, which is why the timings looked random: with a marker in place, or on a rung that names no order, the same opus takes 2.4 s to 2.5 s (three runs, the main checkout, W-413's review marker held: `prof/w413-a.out`), because `silence` is never called. W-413's branch length and logs cost nothing measurable: whatever `next <opus>` loads, records included, is inside those 2.3 s. `next --all` is another matter (Out of scope): it never calls `silence`.

**The design.** The walk counts the entries it lists, and at `SILENCE_WALK_ENTRIES` (100000) it stops. A stopped walk answers one more note on the existing health line: `no step recorded (silence not judged: past 100000 entries, stopped in <dir>)`. Nothing else moves. It is not stalled, because a partial walk proves no silence; it is not activity, because a fresh file it happened to list proves nothing about W-316's fail-closed reads of the rest; and it is not `unknown`, which holds the opus at exit 1, because nothing has failed. The line stays a notice, as W-316 made it: the step named and the exit status are the same as for `no step recorded`. Inside the budget every answer is W-316's, byte for byte.

Why this and not the others:

- **A cache of the newest mtime** is the trap: a stall line that reads stale state tells the orchestrator a working agent has stopped. Nothing here is cached.
- **Stopping at the first fresh file** answers the active case only. The slow case is the silent one, where `stalled` needs every file read, and it would let a fresh file hide a later failed read that W-316-b5 pins as `unknown`.
- **A depth cap on the evidence directories** gives an answer every time, but a file changing below the cap reads as silence: a false `stalled` is the failure W-316's round 4 removed.
- **Pruning old evidence** (W-380) treats W-413 and not the class: the next prototype's throwaway copies grow it back. It remains worth doing, since it gives W-413 its stall notice back (Limits).

The budget is set from the census: above W-451's 70024, so every opus but W-413 is judged as today, and low enough to stay well inside the measure: the stopped walk cost 0.3 s of the 2.47 s profiled at load 2.6 (`prof/cpu-order-after-2.txt`), and W-451's 70024 entries took 2.36 s at load 10 (`census.out`).

**The measure.** The same lab, the same state, the series tip: 3.74 s, 2.50 s and 2.65 s (`prof/before-after.log`), against 75.4 s, 59.7 s and 47.0 s on master's code, at load 2.5 to 3.0. The 10 s target holds, with room. W-413 is the slowest live opus by a factor of 73 in entries, and the walk is the only per-opus cost that scales this way. No test row asserts a time: the row pins the work avoided (a counted directory past the stop is never listed).

**Overlap.** This opus touches `packages/cli/src/next.ts` (the step-health region and one call site), so it should carry `next_ts` in `studio/queue.yml`; no lane holds another `next_ts` entry today. W-364's branch does not touch `next.ts` at its current tip (`git diff origin/master...opus/W-364`); it edits `next-world.test.ts`, the fixture this opus's row reads and does not edit. W-413's branch does not touch `next.ts` or `docs/NEXT.md`. W-363, the next `next_ts` entry, is not in flight.

Threat model: mistakes, not adversaries (D-045). The directories are the opus's own and the seam is a test seam.

Promise budget: report-mode `next` on a dispatch order with no marker lists at most `SILENCE_WALK_ENTRIES` (100000) entries under the counted directories, counting every listed entry but `node_modules` and `.git`, a directory's own included, the worktrees before the evidence; one entry past them it stops, lists nothing more, and its health line reads `no step recorded (silence not judged: past <n> entries, stopped in <dir>)`, with the step and the exit status those of `no step recorded`, as pinned by `W-502-b1` "one entry past the budget: the walk stops and says it did not judge, never stalled" and pinned by `W-502-b1` "a counted directory past the stop is not listed" and pinned by `W-502-b1` "a fresh file does not make a stopped walk read active" and pinned by `W-502-b1` "the budget's value: above W-451's 70024 entries, the largest honest opus measured" — limit: a stopped walk gives no stall notice, so an opus over the budget that has stopped reads `silence not judged` until its directories shrink; a counted directory or file past the stop that cannot be read is not reached, so it does not read `unknown`; one directory's listing is read whole before its entries are counted, so a single directory of millions of entries still costs its listing; the time the budget buys is measured on this host (`prof/before-after.log`), not asserted.

Limits, stated:

- **W-413 itself reads `silence not judged`** on a dispatch order until `~/.bisellium-evidence/W-413-spec` shrinks below the budget: its stall notice is off, and it says so.
