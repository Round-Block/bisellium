## Intent

The session handoff's Boot step 4, "Known traps until filed", grew to 16 lines and every session reads it at boot (Patron 11-01). Nothing retires a line: "until W-239", "until W-371" and "Until filed:" are prose, and no rule reads them. Today's master block holds 13 lines. One opens `Standing`, one opens `Until W-239:`, four carry an inline `until W-<n>` or `Until filed:`, and the rest name nothing.

**The design: one marker per line, read by the existing handoff rule (`sessionHandoffFindings`, `packages/cli/src/rules/process.ts`).**

1. **The item.** The Known traps item is the Boot list line matching `^<n>. Known traps` (a numbered item). Its lines run to the next numbered item or `#` heading. Blank lines are skipped, so a blank line cannot hide lines after it. A handoff with no such item is outside the rule. A second one is `process.handoff.input`.
2. **The marker (behaviour 1).** Each line opens `- Standing: ` or `- Until W-<n>: `, then text. `Standing` is a trap no opus retires. `Until W-<n>` names the one opus that retires the trap. Any other line is `process.handoff.trap`: no marker, `Until filed:`, a lower-case `until`, two ids, a note before the colon, an empty text, or a wrapped continuation line. So is a marked line that still holds `until W-<n>` or `until filed` in its text, since a line states its retirement once, in its marker. An `Until` id with no record is `process.handoff.input`, as a Queue id with no record is.
3. **Retirement (behaviour 2).** A line whose `Until` opus is done is `process.handoff.trap`, "W-<n> is done: remove the trap it retired, or re-mark what still stands". A `halted`, `greenlit` or `backlog` opus keeps its line. Ids in a line's text are never read, only the marker.
4. **The cap (behaviour 3).** More than 12 lines is `process.handoff.traps`, at the item's line. Unmarked lines count.

**"Until filed" lines.** The rule refuses them. A trap that waits on work no opus holds is either filed as an opus (then `Until W-<n>:`) or is `Standing`. Master's one such clause is in the stale-handover line: "Until filed: ready does not check a red's pin equals its first failure or the ledger kill". It leaves the block, and the producer files it (Defaults 2).

**Why done, not merged.** `next`'s done step already reads the handoff with the opus it finishes marked done (`handoffHold`, `packages/cli/src/next.ts`, reused). It holds the step while any `process.handoff.*` finding stands. So finishing W-372 holds until its trap line is removed or re-marked. The lane that lands the fix retires the trap, and nobody remembers it later. The W-299 rule that "making an opus done asks no handoff edit" still holds for the Queue. It does not hold for a trap that names the opus. That is this opus's point.

**The line count.** handoff-check.mjs's whole-file 100-line count is already `process.handoff.lines` (W-186). This opus moves in the count the script never had, the trap block's own 12-line cap. The script's other checks (bytes, line length, the in-flight line) stay in the script (Out of scope).

**Today's master handoff does not pass.** With the rule alone, `check studio --repo .` on master a1ec75f7 blocks 12 lines and the cap (`flips.out`). The opus's own behaviour-1 commit rewrites the block (`traps-master.txt` to `traps-tip.txt`) to 12 marked lines, and `check` then passes on it (`gates-proto.out`).

## Interfaces

- `process.handoff.trap` (new rule id, block): a Known traps line without its marker, with a second retirement in its text, or naming a done opus. Its `where` is `docs/SESSION-HANDOFF.md:<line>`.
- `process.handoff.traps` (new rule id, block): the item over 12 lines, at the item's line, "the Known traps item holds <n> lines; the cap is 12".
- `process.handoff.input` (existing): also an `Until` id with no record, and a second Known traps item.
- `trapLines(lines, from): number[]` (new, not exported): the 0-based non-blank lines after the item at `from`, up to the next line matching `^(\d+\.\s|#)`.
- The marker grammar: `^- (?:Standing|Until (W-\d+)): \S`, case kept. The retirement words refused after it: `until W-<n>` or `until filed`, any case, bounded by a non-letter, non-mark, non-digit.
- Unchanged: `sessionHandoffFindings(text, opera)`'s signature, `HandoffInputs`, `handoffInputs`, `checkSessionHandoff`, `handoffHold`, every other `process.handoff.*` rule.

Limits, stated:

- Unchanged code the promises lean on is a stated limit, not a guarantee (D-076 E): `handoffInputs` (the opera read and its states), `checkSessionHandoff` (the repository-only scope), `checkStudio`, and the done step's `handoffHold` (`packages/cli/src/next.ts`).
- The item is found by its heading. A handoff that drops or renames the `Known traps` item is outside the rule, as an absent handoff is today.
- A `halted` opus never lands, so its trap stands until someone re-marks it. Master's `Until W-239:` line is such a line.
- The rule reads the marker, not the trap's meaning. Whether a trap belongs to its `Until` opus is the writer's call, and the done-step hold makes the lane that lands it look again.
- Boot step 5 ("Standing until their opus lands") is not read.
