## Intent

Put the Officina detail page on the same meaning-based content roles as Home without changing information, order, geometry, copy, reads or controls. The composition is three stacked, unboxed sections. The page title and the Work in flight, Budget and Health headings use `--color-heading` (Dracula Purple `#BD93F9`); lifecycle, collegium and Health category labels use `--color-category` (Dracula Pink); work ids, group counts, Budget numerals, due-owner ids and rule counts use the non-link `--color-fact` role (Dracula Classic Cyan). Content links and visited content links use `--color-link` (the same Classic Cyan) with a persistent underline; navigation keeps its existing separate treatment.

Ordinary titles and explanations remain on `--color-text-primary`, `--color-text-secondary` and `--color-text-muted`. W-427 severity remains separate from content accents: Budget normal/over and Health normal/blocking/high/low retain the hollow circle, square, diamond and triangle cues and the existing severity roles. No heading, category, fact or link role communicates state. Evidence for the role bindings is `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-467-spec/r5/clone/apps/web/src/themes/dracula.css`; the later Patron ruling is `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-467-spec/r5/clone/studio/decisions/D-217.md`.

Every colour declaration W-467 adds consumes a W-675 `--color-*` role. The new stylesheet has no literal, Dracula palette variable, legacy alias or custom-property definition. W-675's compatibility aliases in unchanged `w025.css` still resolve inherited severity, surface and interaction colours through the same theme layer until D-217 item 8's later consumer-migration child; that unchanged code is a limit, not a second palette source.

D-217 item 10 exceptions touched here remain exceptions, not claims of full Dracula compliance: secondary and muted text are the recorded off-palette `--color-text-secondary` / `--color-text-muted` values, and the unchanged new-work brake still uses the recorded verdigris action role. The derived blocking-text shade, pink text selection, Fasti verdigris and dark shadows are not assigned by W-467. The console is not described as fully Dracula-compliant.

Revision 5 has no W-503 dependency. Its prototype is six W-467 commits directly after master `74bc14b3`; it carries none of W-503's six commits. The Home match is role semantics ruled by D-217 and implemented by W-675, not W-503's parked composition. Limit: W-467 does not claim that bare master's Home has W-503's cards, bars, states or content; it changes only Officina's role assignments.
