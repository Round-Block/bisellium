## Intent

Put the Officina detail page on the same content palette as Home without changing its information, order, geometry, reads or controls. Work in flight, Budget and Health remain three stacked, unboxed detail sections. Page and section headings become white `--ink`; lifecycle, collegium and Health category labels become pink `--accent-pink`; work ids, counts, Budget numerals, due-owner ids and rule counts become cyan `--accent-cyan`; ordinary titles and explanations keep the `--ink-2` and `--ink-3` neutral hierarchy. Existing Officina links retain the cyan link role. No accent communicates a state.

Master's W-451 foundation supplies durable work links and current-page focus and reuses the already-shared cyan link, pink selection, purple heading and neutral roles; it explicitly adds no Home route or Home palette surface. Evidence is `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-467-spec/w503-brief-current.md`, `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-503-spec/prototype-r10.mbox` and `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/W-503-spec/shots/home-needs-1280-expanded.png`.
