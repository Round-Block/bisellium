## Intent

Keep Officina as the console's operational detail page, not its landing page. W-435 owns Home, the future default route, first navigation position and cross-screen overview. W-408 leaves master's `Inbox, Board, Seats, Officina` order and Inbox fallback unchanged.

Officina answers three detailed questions in order. Work in flight shows each active opus once under its lifecycle phase—Review, Building or Blocked—and each row names who moves next from a pending gate or, when no gate is pending, the assigned sella. Budget shows one shared-palette spent-versus-allowance pie for each collegium: neutral ink/hollow circle in the normal state while ordinarily under allowance, Orange/diamond while approaching or exactly spent, and Red/square with exact `+N over` past allowance. The visible monospaced numerals and shape keep the state available without colour, beside the posture word and its factual reason. Health shows Lex status and Process health as ordinary-size disclosures with the same W-427 vocabulary and an explicit new-work brake.

The Patron's 2026-10-28 rulings supersede the agent-written Officina prohibition on pies and the earlier 4px burn rule. They are recorded in `docs/design/DIRECTION.md` §6: the section is named Budget; the spent slice uses the shared severity palette rather than white and the remainder is a muted track; exactly spent is Orange/diamond while over is Red/square plus overage; the pie is never the sole carrier of the fraction; Health uses the same token/shape vocabulary; the brake says “Stop new work”/“Resume new work” and explains its boundary; Work in flight says it is grouped by lifecycle phase while retaining row-level mover labels. Existing `daily`, `traditio` and other specialist labels remain unchanged for W-438.

The flat ledger treatment still makes sense: unlike facts are separated by hairlines without boxes, tiles or repeated decoration. The agent-written fasti and all “as of”/generated-at lines remain absent. W-407's single visible-refresh helper keeps the four reads current. One always-allocated shell scrollbar serves the page; no descendant becomes a vertical scroller.

## Defaults for the Patron

- Budget's approaching threshold defaults to **80%** through one exported constant. Below it the ordinary state is normal with neutral ink/hollow circle; 80% through below allowance and exactly spent use high Orange/diamond; over uses blocking Red/square plus exact `+N over`. The threshold is a one-constant Patron change.
- Every Budget pie uses its shared token for the spent slice and the muted rule for the remainder; there is no white spent slice. The shape sits beside the visible numerals, whose accessible name also states within/approaching/fully spent/over allowance.
- A pending gate is the most precise mover label (`patron pending`, `ci pending`); with none, the assigned sella is shown as `builder next`. A missing sella remains blank rather than guessed.
- Keep classified severity cues on the aggregate totals. Show unclassified rule-family names and counts without severity symbols, with a short explanation that severity is available only in aggregate. A richer wire can add a local per-family mapping later.
- Shape vocabulary is blocking square, high diamond, normal hollow circle and low triangle. Comment low is only 2.506:1 on wash, so its ink edge and triangle are required; the adjacent words/counts stay ordinary ink.
- The brake starts available inside collapsed Process health. Its explanation is always inside the disclosure; stopping prevents new pieces from starting and does not claim to interrupt running work.
- Officina remains fourth in master's side panel. W-435 decides Home placement and landing behavior.
- The page has no fasti, “as of,” relative age or generated-at line. One W-407 helper refreshes the four detail reads every 30 seconds while visible and on focus.
- The full main-pane width, hairlines and one shell scrollbar remain; no boxes, zebra fills or repeated decoration mark every row the same.
