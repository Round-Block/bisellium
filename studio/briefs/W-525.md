## Intent

No verb lifts a halt. `next` holds a halted record, so it issues no step ticket; `ready` needs a ticket and a build brief. So W-456 (a finished study) and W-157 (`decide`, ranked in the Queue) are stuck, and W-527, a finished study in backlog, has no way to `done`: `done` asks a gate, a merged branch and a ticket a study never has. D-187 says each study's record waits for this opus.

After it: `bisellium lift <opus> --reason <text> --decision <id>` takes a record out of `halted`, and `bisellium conclude <opus> --from <report> --decision <id> --sella <id>` closes a study. `next` names each at the record that needs it.

**Decided: one verb, `lift`, shaped as `halt` and `waive` are.** Off the ladder, no ticket, `--reason`, `--decision`, `--sella`, `--studio`, `--now`, because `next` will not issue a ticket for a halted record and that is the problem. `resume` is taken: it un-pauses the studio. It writes the state, deletes the four halt fields as `ready` does, and appends one `amendments:` row: `{ at, sella, field: halt, reason, decision, superseded }`, with the whole halt it undid in `superseded`. That is who, when, why, against what decision, and what was lifted, on the record, in the list the record already keeps. No new front-matter key, and no Patron timeline line (W-526's lesson: the producer runs this).

**Decided: it goes back to `greenlit`, or to `building` for a record past ready.** A halt does not record where it came from. `ready`'s own test (W-166) reads it: `start` or `builder_runtime` means past ready. All 164 halted records have neither today, so `building` is only a future's path, and it is pinned.

**Decided: what a lift asks, in order, each refusing with nothing written.**

- *The record is halted, its halt is whole, and it is not terminal.* The row writes down the halt it undid, so a missing or blank `reason`, `resume_when` or `halted_by` refuses and is not recorded as `undefined`; all 164 are whole (`census-halts.out`). W-042 made abandon "a halt whose `resume_when` is `never`": 60 of the 164. A terminal halt holds, and the work is a new opus. W-239 and W-435, the two records under `queue.yml`'s `halted:`, are both terminal (Defaults 2).
- *A Patron's ruling, naming the opus, dated after the halt.* `patronDecisionProblem` already reads a Patron's decision, a whole-token name and a time; this adds where the name must be: at or below `## Ruling` or `## Decree`. That is not fussiness. Every hand-written decision opens "there is still no `decide` verb (W-157)" above its ruling, so a bare `mustName` would let D-178 lift W-157: the very decision that says those records stay halted. This is the guard that keeps W-073, W-438 and W-136 halted: no decision after their 11-03 halt names them at all (`real-records.out`, every decision since, each refused). They stay until a ruling names them. W-456 lifts on D-187, and W-157 on D-182 item 6.
- *A declared milestone and value.* `check` skips a halted record's (`opus.milestone`) and blocks a non-halted one's, and 14 of the 164 have none. So `lift` asks first, and names `amend`.
- *A rank in a queue's `next`, where `queue.yml` exists.* D-174 item 2 halted the unranked; a lift that puts one back undoes it. It also means nothing under `halted:`, `cut:` or `deferred:` lifts until the line moves. The Queue moves first, by hand, as it does now. So W-073, W-136 and W-438 are refused a second way, unranked. `readQueue` lives in `packages/cli`, so `main.ts` hands the verb `liftQueueRefusal`, the shape `done` has for `mergeRefusal`.

**Who may lift.** Nothing rules a seat. D-178 and D-174 item 2 say when those records resume, and the production lex lets production halt. So the ruling is what is Patron's, and any seat can type the command (Defaults 1). It must say who: unlike `halt`, no name does not fall back to `guest`, since who lifted is what the row is for (`namedSella`, as `amend` and `red` read it).

**Decided: `conclude`, a second verb, and not `done`, not a build brief.** W-344's title says it: research "never takes the build ladder". Three shapes were read.

- *`halt --resume-when "never: complete"`*, as W-344 did. No code, but the record is `halted` and not `done`, its value leaves the milestone (`completion.ts`, `milestones.ts` skip halted), and the report is nowhere the record names. The opus asks for `done`.
- *`done` with kind-specific gates* (`effectiveProbationes` returning none for research). `done` still needs a ticket and a merge, so `next` needs a new rung. And it opens a hole: `verify` and `next` would then pass any record filed as research with no gates. Rejected.
- *A narrow verb, off the ladder, that `check` reads by a narrow predicate.* Chosen. `concludedResearch(r)`: kind research, state done, neither `start` nor `builder_runtime`, a `concluded_by`. Anything else that is done keeps every gate, pinned both ways. `conclusionProblem` then asks of that candidate what a study has instead: the report under `acta/`, naming the opus, and the ruling. It is the one validator: `check` blocks on it as `research.report`, `owedRetros` refuses on it, and only a valid conclusion is exempt, so the two cannot disagree.

**What it records.** The report is copied into `acta/<date>-report-<opus>.md`, `kind: consultation`, `opus: <id>`, like a retro's `acta/<date>-retro-<id>.md`. It has to move: `~/.bisellium-evidence/` is outside the repository, and a record names nothing there (D-008). `acta/` is one of the directories `land` carries. The record gets `end`, `report` and `concluded_by`. W-456's and W-527's reports were both written through and checked on a copy (`real-records.out`).

**From `backlog` or `greenlit`, never `halted`.** W-527 sits in backlog, and D-187 names it, which is more than a greenlight says. W-456 is lifted first, one more command in the same land, so a terminal halt (W-344) cannot be concluded by accident. A record with a `start` or a `builder_runtime` has taken the ladder, and refuses: `tookLadder`, the one reading of that for `lift`, `conclude`, `concludedResearch` and `next`.

**No retro is owed.** D-039 section 2: a retro "reads the opus's recorded review verdicts and reds". A study has neither, and `retro --opus` would hold every new opus at `branch` until a triage of nothing is filed. `owedRetros` exempts a valid conclusion and refuses an invalid one (Defaults 3).
