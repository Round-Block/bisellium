## Intent

Make Home the console's default route and first shared navigation entry. It is a compact factual landing page, not a second queue, a work-in-flight screen or a dashboard of invented measures. At desktop width, the large `Needs you` card starts at the left and `Stuck` then `Health` stack to its right. Populated attention groups share the available card width, using at most two columns at 1280 and one below 960; group precedence and every ask label stay intact. Below 960 CSS px, those status cards follow Needs you. One full-width `Work in progress` card follows when active work exists. This is D-175 piece 2; Queue, Completion, Spend and Time do not appear.

Needs you reuses D-151's already-shared attention collection. It never manufactures a second membership or ordering system. A unique attention identity appears once under the first applicable group: `Greenlights`, then `Human gates`, then `Inbox decisions`, then `Questions` for linked or standalone questions without a Board entry. A combined row still prints all of its recorded ask kinds. Board-owned rows link to the existing Board drawer; replyable and orphan question rows link to Inbox. Zero attention retains the one useful top card with `Nothing waiting on you`; it does not render empty group shells.

Stuck counts each opus with at least one failed gate once, excluding lifecycle phases done and halted; its caption is `Work items with failed gates`. Health is the already-served blocking count when non-zero, otherwise the advisory count. Health uses the shared severity cues: blocking square, high advisory diamond and healthy hollow circle. Work in progress groups only populated lifecycle phases other than backlog, planned, done and halted, in the lifecycle's declared phase order. It is words and counts, with no bar, chart, time claim or decorative motion. Zero active work omits the entire lower card.
