## Intent

Make Home the console's default route and first shared navigation entry. It is a compact factual landing page, not a second queue, a work-in-flight screen or a dashboard of invented measures. At desktop width, the large `Needs you` card starts at the left and `Stuck` then `Health` stack to its right. D-200 restores the signed round-6 Needs you treatment: three desktop group columns, uppercase mono labels and explicit `Open`; one populated group still spans the card, while a fourth wraps and all groups use one column below 960. Below 960 CSS px, those status cards follow Needs you. One full-width `Work in progress` card follows when active work exists. This is D-175 piece 2 as corrected by D-200; Queue, Completion, Spend and Time do not appear.

Needs you reuses D-151's already-shared attention collection. It never manufactures a second membership or ordering system. A unique attention identity appears once under the first applicable group: `Greenlights`, then `Human gates`, then `Inbox decisions`, then `Questions` for linked or standalone questions without a Board entry. A combined row still prints all of its recorded ask kinds; `Open` exposes the row's existing Board or Inbox destination without adding an action. D-200 accepts the mostly blank zero-attention card: it retains `Nothing waiting on you` and renders no empty group shells.

Stuck counts each opus with at least one failed gate once, excluding lifecycle phases done and halted; its caption is `Work items with failed gates`. Health is the already-served blocking count when non-zero, otherwise the advisory count. Health uses the shared severity cues: blocking square, high advisory diamond and healthy hollow circle. D-200 restores the signed colours: Home/card/status headings are white, a blocking number is red and a high-advisory number is amber. Work in progress groups only populated lifecycle phases other than backlog, planned, done and halted, in the lifecycle's declared phase order; D-200 restores one proportional bar per populated phase, with width `count / total active work`. Zero active work omits the entire lower card. No time claim, decorative chart or motion is added.
