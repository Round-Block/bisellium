Patron 11-01: 117 worktrees and 15 GB had piled up and cost every session's boot context. The done perform removes every worktree of its opus (opus, spec-scratch, stage, verify, kills/reds/series); a records PR's worktree goes once the PR merges; tick sweeps worktrees whose branch is merged or whose opus is done, saving a dirty one's diff first. Never a worktree younger than an hour (a fresh branch at master looks merged: the 11-01 sweep removed a live records worktree) or one with an open PR. Stale .git/worktrees admin dirs are cleared (the busy-delete case, W-227). Model: ~/.bisellium-evidence/cleanup-1101/.
