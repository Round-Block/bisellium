The Patron, 2026-11-09: 'Talk goes to relevant leads, not all.' A D-189 meet asks the leads relevant to the call, each named with why it is relevant; picking them is the producer's deliberate job (the 11-09 miss: the design lead was relevant to both calls and was not asked). Enforced in code, not memory: a bisellium meet verb takes a question file and one or more --lead <seat>=<why relevant>, dispatches each named seat through its harness (talk for Codex seats) and no other, and writes the question, the named leads with their reasons, the answers and the producer's labelled view under studio/acta/meets/<date>-<slug>/. A lead with no reason is refused; there is no all-leads default. A check rule refuses a D-189 default (a queue line, filing or amendment citing a meet) whose meet directory lacks a reason or an answer for a lead it names. Red rows: a meet with no --lead refused; a --lead with no reason refused; exactly the named seats dispatched; check refuses a cited meet missing a named lead's reason or answer. Supersedes the all-five draft filed and discarded 11-09. Exempt from D-178's freeze at the Patron's ask.
