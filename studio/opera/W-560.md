The Patron's call 2026-11-09 (W-547): a failed `ci --opus` leaves its gate logs uncommitted in the worktree while the record names them; when the ladder re-enters the spec rung (the D-085 class loop), the spec perform refuses them as changed beside the spec, and no verb clears them: setting them aside breaks the next ci's check (link.dead), and the guard (W-364) rightly refuses a hand move, so the Patron had to rule a builder restore. Rule: the verb that produces evidence commits it, pass or fail; a failed ci commits its own logs as a passing one does through the gates commit, so every later rung starts from a clean tree. Red rows: after a failed ci --opus the worktree is clean and the record's gate logs are committed; a spec perform after a failed ci is not refused for those logs. Lesson filed with it: a guard refusal that ends in the Patron running a step by hand is a missing verb, filed as an opus at once. Evidence ~/.bisellium-evidence/W-547-build/ci4/, ci-opus-5.log.
