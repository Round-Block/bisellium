## Context

D-061 §6 makes greenlight the one Patron gate; `greenlight --auto` (W-403, basis named by D-114 via bisellium.yml auto_greenlit_by) covers only D-061 §4 remainders and D-076 D cause fixes, so an ordinary small engineering bug fix such as W-673 waited on the Patron, though the Patron's standing line is that only UI/UX and architecture are human-only.

## Ruling

1. W-673 is greenlit by the Patron (recorded with `bisellium greenlight W-673`).
2. D-061 §6 is amended: an opus is greenlit without the Patron when it is not kind ui, its collegium is engineering, ops or production (process), it is sized at 3 points or fewer, and its title names no security, sandbox, branch protection, architecture or code-owned path; ui opera, architecture, opera over 3 points or unsized, and those exceptions keep the Patron's gate.
3. Until greenlight --auto implements item 2 (an engineering opus to be filed), the producer may run `bisellium greenlight <opus>` for an opus meeting item 2 and names this decision in the land title; the Patron vetoes at the checkpoint.

## Limits

The code-owned review on the landing PR (studio/decisions/ is code-owned) stays the Patron's.
