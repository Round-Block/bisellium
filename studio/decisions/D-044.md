## Decree

1. **Security fixes are mandatory.** A CodeQL or code-scanning alert, or a
   security-review finding, at any severity, is fixed before merge; it is
   never dismissed as a false alarm to save a round. The review rounds and
   receipts the fix needs are pre-approved beyond the five-round cap; the
   producer reports them at the next checkpoint.
2. **UI/UX design on Codex.** Every opus with a console audience (D-041)
   gets a ui-lead pass (`gpt-6-astra`) that drafts its layout and the UI
   defaults for the Patron to veto, before the architect signs the spec.
