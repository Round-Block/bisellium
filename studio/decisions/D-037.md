## Decree

The rule of D-016 stands. Its "Out" list is read through the rule: `add`,
`commit`, `push`, `rebase` and `gh pr merge` stay out as verbs of their own.
`next` performs them only as the one rung its derivation names, and refuses
in every other state (W-124, W-141). The CLI never moves a Patron path in
the main checkout; it stops and prints the Patron's command.
