## Decree

1. **A spec review before the build.** After the architect signs a spec and
   before `branch`, Codex (`gpt-5.6-sol`) reviews the brief once against two
   questions only: (a) does its input section name every record the opus
   reads, with a real rejecting function that fails closed (W-161); (b) can
   each promise actually be guaranteed, or does it need a stated limit (the
   threat model is mistakes, not adversaries, D-045)? A finding on either
   blocks the spec until the architect revises it; anything else is advisory.
2. **Three rounds, then the Patron.** A build review round past the third
   needs the Patron's OK; after a third failed round the producer stops and
   asks, with a recommendation (one more fix round, or a re-spec). A re-spec
   does not restart the count. Security-fix rounds stay pre-approved (D-044).
   *Superseded by D-053 (Patron, 2026-10-10): no round cap.*
3. **Enforcement.** W-162 makes `next` require the spec review and stop
   after a third failed round; until then the producer applies both by hand.
