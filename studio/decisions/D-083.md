## Decree

A. **Later.** The handoff Queue's Later list holds filed opera that are not scheduled. Nothing in Later starts unless
   (1) a re-rank moves it into Now with a minutes-saved estimate (a re-rank runs at an early stop, at the block's exit,
   or when Now holds fewer than 5 items), or (2) it turns high under D-039 (security, data loss, or a mistake class seen
   twice), which starts it at once. Each re-rank also reviews Later: an item whose premise is stale is re-scoped or
   halted, never left to rot.
B. **W-288 is amended and greenlit, split at spec.** `docs/ADOPTION.md` keeps the contract (vocabulary and layout, the
   manifest, seats, the lifecycle, record formats, the source tree hash, the review gates) and stays code-owned; six
   per-command reference files (`docs/CHECK.md`, `NEXT.md`, `PIPELINE.md`, `VERBS.md`, `SERVE.md`, `TALK.md`) leave code
   ownership; a blocking `doc.cap` check rule reads `doc_caps` in `studio/bisellium.yml` (700 lines for ADOPTION, 450 for
   any other `docs/*.md`; part 1 lands ADOPTION's cap at its moved size and part 2 lowers it). Nothing is generated from
   the CLI (the `bisellium` usage banner is the generated reference). The W-254 and W-248 dependency is dropped. Part 1
   (2 points: the scripted move, the ownership narrowing, the cap, the pinned tests' path flips) goes in the Queue right after W-275; part 2
   (3 points: ids and round narrative out, every limit kept as one present-tense sentence, the false-positive and
   false-negative lists shrunk to one Limits line naming the brief, flag lists the banner duplicates out) goes to Later.
   Part 1 is exempt from the process cap as process-removing (D-061 §5).
