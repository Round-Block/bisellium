   (`ui-spec-reviewer`) for design. The design lex's mandate to spec every opus narrows to design's own opera.

   `gpt-5.6-sol` build review in a fresh session each round (D-066 C), the merge queue's `certify`, done. Ops adds:

   records (ops lex §2); a change to certify's host runner is split out and lands first (W-323); a `.github/` change
   goes to the Patron (ops lex §4). Design keeps the UI flow (D-091 A, D-099): Terra builds, a fresh Sol session
   reviews, the Patron rules on the screens, `verify` runs on master.
C. **Production owns the ladder's rules, records and sequencing, and the ladder's tooling code** (the Patron chose
   this of three scopings, 2026-10-29): `next`, `verdict`, the record verbs, `review-packet.mjs` and the reds replay;

   signed by a new seat, `ladder-lead` (Claude Opus 5.5, collegium production), never by `producer`: the orchestrator
   does not sign specs for the ladder it runs.
D. **Five lanes, one per lead.** The Patron: "production and qa will have their own lanes". Engineering, Ops,
   Production, QA and Design (two UI lanes, as today). The Queue keeps one list per lane once the re-tag lands; lanes
   open in stages within the usage allowance, the orchestrator naming each opening to the Patron.
E. **Enforced by W-452.** Its scope widens: `next` names the collegium lead's seat as the spec signer, the manifest
   gains `ladder-lead`, and the lex amendments (design §1, production §1 and §2, each lex naming its spec role) ride
