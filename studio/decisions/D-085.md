Written by the producer at the Patron's instruction (2026-10-19). W-178 (the test-row count check) failed build review
four rounds running, each on a new edge of its runner-import grammar (a comment, a cross-paired line, a member call, a
Unicode binding), about an hour a round. The Patron asked whether this was systemic, had one Fable and one Astra agent
analyse it, and a second Fable agent compile them (`~/.bisellium-evidence/review-loop-1019/`: `brief.md`, `report.md`,
`report-astra.md`, `compiled.md`). Both found the cause in the brief: it says the counter works on files that "import"
the runner without listing which import forms count, so each round the reviewer may lawfully name a new one, and nothing
stops the loop. W-221 looped five rounds the same way and passed once its brief stated the limit (D-059). 10 of 115
reviewed opera had three or more failed build rounds. The Patron adopted the compiled proposal as recommended.

## Decree

A. **The class-loop hold.** Two consecutive failed build-review rounds whose blockers all carry one class, recorded
   against the same brief blob, end the fix loop; so do three consecutive failed build-review rounds of any classes.
   No builder is dispatched: the architect amends the brief to state the limit (the forms the rule recognises; every
   other form a limit), re-signs on the opus branch, and review resumes against the amended brief in the same session.
   The architect may instead answer that the form is inside the stated domain, and the builder then fixes it. A
   security gap or missing or false evidence is exempt and goes to the builder as today. Filing a cause-fix opus does
   not discharge the hold. This is routing, not a cap: D-053 stands.
B. **Who answers the hold.** The architect, by default; the Patron may veto the amendment at the checkpoint.
C. **W-297 is amended and re-ranked right after W-179.** `next` holds a fix round when the last two
   `ci/<id>-review-*.log` share a `[class:` tag and a `# brief:` blob, or the last three failed; an accepted answer is a
   newer architect signature on the opus branch; `verdict` refuses a build finding without `[severity:]`. Until it
   lands the orchestrator applies A by hand (D-066 A).
D. **W-322 is amended.** An Input domain row whose valid domain is any text or any string, for a lexical or
   classifying rule, lists the forms the rule recognises (or cites the Interfaces lines that do) and names every other
   form a limit; the signature's ledger check refuses the row without that line. Ordinary edits in a listed form (a
   comment, a string, a dirty line) stay inside the domain.
E. **D-084 is re-cut, not closed.** Build review blocks on wrong behaviour on an input inside the brief's stated domain
   (a listed form, or an ordinary mistake in one), a security gap (anything touching tickets, authorisation, the
   sandbox or record writes), or missing or false evidence; a form the brief's limits exclude or do not list is an
   advisory. #574 merges only if its replay against the sealed key demotes no finding the key labels plausible.
F. **Kept scripts.** The fix census (`codex-passes/fix-census.sh`) extends only inside the brief's stated domain and
   puts paths that run master's tooling under `## Not judged`, never open. The daily retro reads every review and spec
   log of the day, done or not, flags a loop as in A, and lists classes seen in more than one opus.
G. **W-178 now.** The architect amends its brief with the recognised import forms and re-signs; then review round 5.
