Stated directly by the Patron for the current implementation (web I and the
W-022/W-023 mechanism opera): Claude Opus 4.6 (`claude-opus-4-6`) and Claude
Sonnet 4.6 (`claude-sonnet-4-6`) are used ALONGSIDE the 5 family, not instead
of it:

- Builders split by sella: builder-a and builder-b stay on
  `claude-sonnet-5`; builder-c and builder-d run `claude-sonnet-4-6`
  (manifest `sellae` is the binding, as always).
- Review keeps D-012's one-Opus-reviewer-per-opus, but a two-opus cascade
  splits generations: one opus reviewed on `claude-opus-5`, the other on
  `claude-opus-4-6` — cross-generation review diversity catches failure
  classes a same-model reviewer shares.
- Priced facts, on the record: Sonnet 4.6 is $3/$15 per MTok versus Sonnet
  5's $2/$10; Opus 4.6 matches Opus 5 at $5/$25. The mix is bought for
  diversity, not cost — which is why the kill condition reads on retro
  outcomes.
- The usage events already record `--model` per agent, so the retro's
  by-model section is the evidence this decision lives or dies by.
