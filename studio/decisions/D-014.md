Amended again 2026-09-19 (Patron): opus-tier roles move from
`claude-opus-4-6` to `claude-opus-5`. The cross-generation clause is
struck --- both tiers are now generation 5, so review independence rests
on role separation (a distinct reviewing sella that did not write the
code), not on a model-generation difference. The `process.cascade` check
rule already enforces that separation mechanically; `process.review_tier`
(in build) extends it to model tier.

**One review gate.** The studio had two reviewing agent types --- `censor`
(QA collegium, read-only, was Sonnet 5) and `reviewer46` (engineering,
read+write, Opus) --- both claiming the single `review` probatio. The
duplication was not a naming nit: `censor` is the natural word and the only
one listed in the rendered harness section, so the discoverable type was
the cheaper one, and a cascade session picked it for six reviews at the
wrong tier before the mistake was caught.

Resolved: `censor` is the single review and QA gate, on `claude-opus-5`,
booting as `qa-lead` (whose manifest binding moves to Opus 5 with it).
`reviewer46` is a deprecated stub --- it cannot be deleted from a cascade
session because the sandbox holds `.claude/` read-only to the shell, so it
must be removed by hand.

`censor` keeps its read-only toolset (`Read, Bash, Glob, Grep`) by design:
a reviewer holding `Edit` can fix what it finds, which destroys the
independence the gate exists for. Evidence logs go through the shell,
verdicts through `bisellium review`.
