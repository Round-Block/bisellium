## Decree

A. **A real git conflict holds a lane back, not declared-file overlap.** Before review, `next` checks with
   `git merge-tree --write-tree` (its exit code; this host's git 2.43 has no `--quiet`) whether the opus would conflict
   with master or with an opus further along; on a conflict it orders a rebase before review or a wait for the other to
   land, so a reviewed tree is never rebased afterwards. This amends D-087 C's W-169 design ("refuses an opus whose
   files overlap one in flight") and answers W-169's question 2 the other way.
B. **The conflict check is its own small opus**, filed greenlit in Now right after W-275, not folded into W-169's next
   revision.
C. **W-331 (the `next.ts` split by region) moves after the conflict check** and is done as a kept script on a fresh
   master, amending D-087 D's "right after W-169".
D. **Two lanes until the conflict check lands.** The three-lane run of 2026-10-20 was the Patron's memory and collision
   study, not a standing third lane; it was started by the orchestrator before any ruling, and this decree closes it.
   Until the check lands, the orchestrator runs the merge-tree check by hand before each review.
E. **UI-related opera are `kind: ui`.** Console and other UI work is filed, or amended when it starts, as `kind: ui`, so
   the UI policy applies (ui-lead `gpt-6-astra` input at spec, the Patron's ruling, the served-e2e manifest, the
   post-merge `verify` until certify can carry it). No opus has been `kind: ui` before; W-304, W-280, W-074 and W-300
   are `feature`/`task` today.
F. **Four UI checks are filed greenlit:** (1) screenshot baselines (`toHaveScreenshot` at 1280 and 390 per screen,
   generated on the CI runner) and a lint rule refusing hard-coded colours in `apps/web/src` outside `tokens.css`;
   (2) the screenshots, or their pixel diff, in the build review packet with a UI slot in `studio/prompts/build-review.md`,
   after a probe of whether the Codex seat reads images; (3) UI acceptance rows in briefs (each screen as route, viewport
   and what it shows, and its loading, empty, error and refused states, each a served test; W-280 first); (4) the
   `apps/web/README.md` fix (the live screens, the two suites, a pointer to `docs/design/DIRECTION.md`).
G. **No re-rank for today's overruns; their root causes are studied first.** W-334 (~305 min), W-268 part 1 (~230) and
   W-335 (~203) all crossed lex §7's 180-minute early-stop line on the day a re-rank (#617) had already run. The
   Patron: a re-rank would not help; the root causes of the overruns are examined before any rule change, and the
   study is reported to the Patron first.
H. **W-268 part 1's third spec review** (`ci/W-268-spec-6.log`), past D-061 §4's two-review cap, ran at the Patron's
   explicit word after a build-time flip; a one-off, not a precedent.
