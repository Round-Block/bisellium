## Decree

A. **Measure.** Cycle minutes, from the first spec signature (`ci/<id>-spec-1.log`) to the done PR landed, is primary;
   lead minutes (from greenlight) and Patron wait are reported beside it; record-writing hand steps (D-073's count) are a
   secondary line with no stop rule. `~/.bisellium-evidence/speed-1017/elapsed.mjs` computes it; every checkpoint row
   leads with it.
B. **Exit and stop.** The block's exit: cycle under 150 min on two consecutive opera after W-292, one of them carrying a
   failed review round, with the trial's other conditions (no hand-run evidence scripts, no required handoff edits,
   review independence kept, a safe restart after an interruption). Early stop and re-rank: a cycle over 180 min after
   W-292, or two calendar weeks. Target after W-179 and W-259: under 90.
C. **Order.** W-303 (and W-264) → W-214 → W-274 → W-292 → W-179 (with W-178) → W-259 → W-258 → W-305 (fast lane) →
   W-182 → W-183 (the slot wrapper and waiting line only) → W-169 → the rest of the block. D-061 §5's exemption holds:
   W-179 and W-259 remove waiting.
D. **Fast lane (W-305).** A `Brief: light` brief with one behaviour, at most three Files owned and no path W-255 refuses,
   signed by the architect, goes to `branch` with no Codex spec round; the Codex build review stays the one gate.
E. **One builder per opus.** Reds are recorded one behaviour at a time on the previous behaviour's commit (D-036), and
   each builder clone pays the full suite, so several builders on one opus save under 10 min. Concurrency comes from lanes.
F. **A second lane after W-292 and W-179, before the console MVP** (replaces D-071 L): W-182 → W-183 → W-169. Heavy-job
   slots on this host: 1 until W-179 lands, then 2, measured on the first two-lane opus.
G. **Timestamps.** Gate logs gain `# at:` stamps and the mint receipt's end is tracked; this rides W-259.
