Written by the producer at the Patron's instruction (2026-10-19), on the compiled Fable and Astra audit of where an
opus's cycle minutes go (`~/.bisellium-evidence/spec-build-1019/compiled.md`, section 3). The audit found that the full
suite (about 18 minutes a run, one per attempt) and extra review rounds carry the cycle, not the builder (2 to 5
minutes); W-179's spec round 6 (`studio/ci/W-179-spec-6.log`) failed on two blockers in the architect's evidence
scripts whose fixes keep the design, and so sent the brief back for a re-sign and a second review (45 to 90 minutes);
W-322's fix check ran after its fix round's suite, found a missing test, and cost a second 18-minute run. D-076 B
already allows a second spec review only after a design-changing S1; nothing made that checkable.

## Decree

A. **A blocking spec-review finding carries `[design: changes]` or `[design: keeps]`.** `changes` means its fix
   changes a behaviour, an interface, a Files owned entry or a promise; anything else is `keeps`. A failed spec round
   whose blockers all say `keeps` goes to `branch`, with those findings as required build fixes: the build packet lists
   them and the build review blocks until each is closed. A `changes` blocker returns the brief to the architect as
   today. The tag is the reviewer's (Codex stays the single gate, D-066 C).
B. **A required fix in an evidence or acceptance script outside the source tree carries no red**; the build review
   judges it from the script's kept outputs.
C. **A blocking finding tagged S2, S3 or S4 is not recorded as a blocker**: the orchestrator sends it back to the same
   reviewer session to fix the tag or the word, and nothing is downgraded automatically (one of W-322's S2 findings
   hid a real defect).
D. **The fix check runs before a fix round's `ci --opus`**: the builder stops after its fix commits, the orchestrator
   runs `fix-census.sh`, and only a check with no open path goes on to the suite.
E. **A re-specced opus's cycle counts from its first signature** (W-178, W-179); the span from its newest signature is
   reported beside it.
F. **Where it lives:** `studio/prompts/spec-review.md` and `studio/prompts/later-round.md` (A, C); lex §9 steps 2
   and 4. Until W-297 (A, C) and W-309 (D) enforce it, the orchestrator applies it by hand. It governs from the next
   spec or build round reviewed after it lands on master.
