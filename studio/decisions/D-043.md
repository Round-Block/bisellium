its fifth review round on an unbounded milestone weight, the Patron ruled

## Decree

1. **One exceptional sixth round for W-153 only.** The five-round cap stands
   for every other opus.
2. **Normalized weights.** Overall = Σ(weightᵢ × pctᵢ) / Σweight. A weight
   must be finite and positive; a total that is zero or not finite gives
   "No estimate". `milestones.shape` keeps reporting weights that do not
   total 100 as a tidiness rule, but the meter no longer depends on it.
