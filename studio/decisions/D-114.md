## Decree

1. **What greenlights itself.** `bisellium greenlight <opus> --auto` greenlights an opus in backlog, with no Patron,
   when it is one of two kinds, each read from records other seats' verbs wrote:
   - a D-061 §4 remainder: its `parent:` is greenlit or done, and two of the parent's spec reviews by the configured
     `spec_reviewer` failed with no passed review between;
   - a D-076 D cause fix: a review class its title names (`<area>×<slug>`) is the `[class:]` tag of a standing
     `[severity: S1]` blocker in the failed reviews of two or more other opera.
2. **No cap.** No daily or weekly count; D-061 §5's process cap is unchanged and still applies to what is in flight.
3. **What stays the Patron's.** A kind ui opus or a ui parent's remainder; a value over 3 points, or none; a title
   naming security, the sandbox, branch protection, architecture, CODEOWNERS or a code-owned path; a security class.
   A spec that later sizes an auto-greenlit opus over 3 points, or touches security, the sandbox or branch protection,
   waits for the Patron before branch (production lex §7, D-061 §6); a code-owned path waits for the Patron's
   code-owner review at the pull request.
4. **Record.** An auto greenlight writes `greenlit_by: D-114` and `greenlit_basis`, emits `workflow.greenlight`
   granted, and writes no Patron timeline line. It is reported at the next checkpoint, where the Patron may halt it.
5. **W-289.** When W-289's refusal lands, it refuses a greenlight the Patron did not run; `greenlight --auto` is not
   the Patron's greenlight and is outside it.
