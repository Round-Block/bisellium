## Ruling

The Patron, verbatim: "instead of going with just the production lead's recommendation, it should be a recommendation based on the meet by relevant leads". Asked about permanence, verbatim: "And what happened to making in persistent and permanent?"

1. A call that would otherwise wait on the Patron is put to a meet of the leads whose area it touches, not all five by default. Their recommendation is the default taken, and work carries on.
2. The call, the leads who met, their recommendation and any split between them are reported to the Patron at the next summary, and the Patron can veto it there. The producer's recommendation alone is not a default.
3. Until `talk` works (W-510, repair talk) a meet is fresh agents in the leads' seats and a Codex session for the design lead, with their answers kept as evidence.

Not changed: a code-owned PR still needs the Patron's approval, which GitHub enforces. Mechanical findings are still fixed without asking. A ladder step with its own gate keeps that gate (D-066 C), and a meet does not replace it.

Not ruled: whether scope, names, a call that contradicts a ruling, and UI and architecture calls may be taken this way. Those go to the Patron as lex section 7 says until the Patron rules. Also not ruled: separating code-owned edits from an opus's PR, and whether a waiting PR stops the lane.
