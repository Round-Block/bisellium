Observed in cascade 1b: a ~361k-token hand edit across many files, where a
small codemod would have done the same work deterministically, reviewably,
and at a fraction of the cost. Recorded here as a decision rather than a
memory so `bisellium retro` can point future cascades at it, and the
Engineering lex now carries it as an enforceable clause (§2, "mechanical,
repeatable work... is written as a script").
