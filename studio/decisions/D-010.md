Adopted alone by the Censor as an advisory detection rule
(`tests.tautological`, advise): an assertion whose expected value is a
literal `true`, an assertion on exit code alone where the command under test
may not exist, and a count hardcoded against a fixture rather than derived
from it, are all reported. The Censor's per-opus mutation step stays
mandatory and its result (`mutationCaught`) is reported per item.

Evidence: lessons/L-007.md (cascade 4b: `report(..., true, ...)` at
instructions.test.ts:293; "exits 0 within 2s" passing against a subcommand
that does not exist; tick.test.ts and server.test.ts hardcoding four
magistri and four collegia), lessons/L-014.md (cascade 1: verifiers that
imported functions instead of spawning the CLI, hiding every exit-code
defect). Three of four items this cascade had a mutation caught; the fourth
(W-019) had nothing to mutate.
