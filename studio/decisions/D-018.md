Decreed 2026-09-20, as part of cascade 23's cleanup (W-038's class: a known
CLI gap — there is no verb to re-stamp identity onto a gate recorded before
the field existed, only `bisellium review --model <id>` for a *new* review
pass).

`certifies: tree:<hash>` did not exist when the earlier gates were signed,
and `review --model` did not exist when the earlier reviews were recorded.
`check` correctly reports both gaps (`probatio.certifies`,
`process.review_tier`) on the pre-W-026 opera — W-016 through W-031 as
listed by check's advisories — because those fields are genuinely absent
from the record. This decision accepts that absence as historical evidence
without identity, rather than a defect to fix by re-signing gates after the
fact (which is exactly the backfill the engineering lex and D-006 forbid:
"evidence is produced, never backfilled").

The advisories stay visible — `check` keeps reporting them, honestly,
against every pre-W-026 opus they apply to. This decision does not silence
the rule; it records that the Patron has looked at the gap and accepted it
for opera whose work is already done and closed.

kill_when: the moment any of these opera is reopened (state moves off
`done`/its halted resting state) or one of its gates is re-recorded, the
grandfathering lapses for that opus — a reopened or re-signed gate owes a
current `certifies`/`model` like any other, not the historical exemption.
