Inferred from the shape of `check.ts` itself, not stated anywhere directly:
`probatio.certifies.stale/.dirty/.mismatch` and `probatio.evidence.tree` all
gate on `ACTIVE.has(state)` (building/verifying/review). A `done` or
`halted` item's certificate is history — the work it attests to already
happened, and re-flagging it every time the tree moves on would be noise
the item can't act on. `since:` (Seam S3, this opus) is the same inference
applied to gate demands: a done opus's gates aren't retroactively judged
against a rule that postdates its handoff.
