## Decree

The Patron, 2026-10-01, reviewing the live Seats screen ("why do we need
fast, mid, high, etc"): **simplify delegation — a munus maps straight to a
seat.** Amends D-023.

## What changes

D-023 made each munus point at a tier and each tier at a model, so one edit
could retarget every munus on a tier. On review the indirection earned
nothing: the Seats screen states changes are "recorded only — they do not yet
affect model dispatch or task routing", the tier models were stale against
the mapping actually in force, and seats already carry models — two sources
of truth for one fact. A munus now names the seat that performs it; the
seat's model (or, after W-089, its template's) is the only model record.

## What does not change

Seats, their models, and W-089's templates and instances. D-014's single
review gate. The munera enumeration itself (aggregation, audit, prereview,
research, redteam, escalation, build, spec, review) stands; only the target
of each mapping moves from tier to seat.

## Migration

The build opus that removes the tier records, re-points each munus at a seat,
and renders delegation as munus → seat is filed through the W-101 allocator
with the other queued filings. Historical delegation events stay as written.
