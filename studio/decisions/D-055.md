## Decree

1. **A collegium of its own.** Ops (id `ops`, magister `ops-lead`) gets its own lex, magister, budget
   and cadence, set up like Chaos (D-052).
2. **Mandate.** The wall-clock time and reliability of the pipeline: CI and the merge queue, receipts and
   verify, flakes, and the host runners. It measures every opus's time per step from the records and
   files an opus for any step past its declared budget. No check is cut to save time (D-054 §1, production
   lex §11).
3. **Owned opera.** W-203, W-205, W-206, W-207, W-183, W-180, W-195 and W-178 move under Ops once the
   collegium exists in the manifest.
4. **Enforcement.** W-208 sets up the collegium; its per-step times feed W-202's reliability section.
