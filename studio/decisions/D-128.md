## Decree

A. **UI work is design's; `kind: ui` retires once W-452 lands.** The Patron (2026-10-29, quoted): "move all ui
   to design lead then" and "we should not have the ui kind". UI work belongs to the design collegium;
   `kind: ui` is retired once W-452 routes the UI flow (Codex Sol architect, Opus spec review, ui-lead input,
   Terra build, the Patron's screen ruling) by `collegium: design`. Until W-452 lands, in-flight UI opera
   (W-407, W-451, W-408 and the Board five) keep `kind: ui`.
B. **Every open opus is re-tagged to the collegium whose lead should own it, by what its task requires**
   (Patron: "there seems to be too many engineering opus"). The clerk reviewed Codex's proposal against the
   five leges; the result is `/home/edckt/.bisellium-evidence/collegium-retag/final.tsv` (production 56,
   ops 44, design 33, engineering 28, qa 7 of 168), applied by a kept script once W-452's `amend --collegium`
   lands, in one records PR. On the two undecided rows the Patron said "go with your rec": W-078 goes to
   production (a new probatio kind is a ladder and records concept) and W-383 goes to engineering (its cause
   fix is a check rule).
C. **Filing names the collegium at the clerk's hand.** "new opus should be categorized correctly as part of
   the workflow for opening new opus"; the Patron said "it should be the clerk's job". The clerk assigns the
   collegium when an opus is filed, with a one-line reason citing the lex; W-452 makes `check` refuse a new
   opus record without a collegium or with `kind: ui`.
D. **Lanes follow leads.** Engineering and Ops become separate lanes (the Patron raised the split on
   2026-10-29; ops opera move off the engineering list once re-tagged). Home's Queue shows one lane per lead
   as W-453, after W-451 lands as signed.
E. **Filed greenlit:** W-452 (production, M3) and W-453 (design, `kind: ui` until W-452, M5).
