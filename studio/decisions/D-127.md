## Decree

1. Home is a card-based dashboard (a bento grid): a top row of count tiles (Needs you, Work in flight, Stuck, Health), then cards: Work in progress, Queue, Budget, Overall completion, Waiting on you (PRs needing the Patron's review or merge), Plan usage, and Time (active running time so far, time for recent work, an estimate to 100% completion). No card repeats a tile. A work item opens its Board drawer. This supersedes, for Home, the 2026-10-10 ruling that the opening page is a ledger, not a dashboard, and DIRECTION §7's no-tiles, no-KPI-strip and no-charts rejects.
2. The Officina's "Posture and spend" becomes "Budget", drawn as one pie per collegium. This supersedes DIRECTION's agent-written "No donut, no gauge" line.
3. One severity palette serves the Inbox (W-427), Health and Budget: blocking Red #FF5555, high Orange #FFB86C, normal uncoloured, low Comment #6272A4, each with a shape cue. Budget: under the allowance normal, approaching and fully spent Orange, over Red with "+N over".
4. Home tracks no "last visit". A "welcome back" summary waits for a sign-in flow: W-441.
5. The data Home needs and lacks is its own work: W-439 (budget records every period), W-440 (history kept for trend charts).
