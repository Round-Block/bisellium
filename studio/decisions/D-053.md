review caps", while W-186's spec review stood at one failed round.

## Decree

1. **No caps.** A failed spec-review, build-review or handoff-review round is followed by a fix and another
   round; the producer no longer stops and asks the Patron after the third. D-046 §2 and D-051 are
   superseded; D-046's spec review itself (§1) stands.
2. **Round-ruling decisions** (D-043, D-048 and the grants in D-051) stay as history; none is needed again.
3. **Enforcement.** W-204 removes `next`'s build-review hold after a third failed round; until it lands
   the producer records a round ruling citing this decree whenever `next` holds on the cap. W-196 no
   longer adds a spec-round hold.
