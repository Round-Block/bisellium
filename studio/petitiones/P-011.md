`docs/research/agent-studio-landscape-2026-09-21.md` ("Proposed Bisellium
direction") proposes: for the first playable delivery, a product-level
acceptance record alongside per-opus acceptance, naming the product
acceptance owner, the release candidate, and the required design,
engineering, art, and QA evidence.

D-014 establishes one review gate — the Censor judges all evidence, on
`claude-opus-5`, alone. A product acceptance sign-off adjacent to it either
(a) is a Patron-authority gate like `greenlight` — a decree checkpoint, not
a review, so D-014 stands untouched; or (b) is a second evidence-judging
review in disguise, which D-014 forbids. The research claims (a) ("a
product decision and sign-off, not a competing review gate"), but the shape
must be decided before anything is specced.

(a) adopt as a Patron gate: a `product acceptance` record kind (like
    `decisions/`), signed only by the named owner (default: the Patron),
    required before a release candidate ships. The Censor still judges all
    evidence; this record judges readiness of the product, a different
    question. Needs a record shape and probably a check rule — a future
    opus this answer can name via `--opus`.
(b) reject: the first playable rides existing machinery — a decision
    (D-nnn) naming the release criteria, closed by the Patron's ordinary
    decree. No new record kind.
(c) defer: the question is real but premature while delivery-blocking
    opera (W-033, W-032, the `run --opus` to `merge` chain) are open.

*(The trade in (a): a second signature on the path to shipping, even one
that judges a different question than the Censor's, is one more place a
release can stall — and "readiness of the product" is a softer line than
the Censor's evidence contract, so its criteria will need to be as
concrete or it becomes a vibe gate next to a mechanical one.)*

Recommendation: (c) — the direction is sound but nothing is scheduled to
accept yet; deciding the record shape now risks specing it against a
first-playable scope that shifts before W-032/W-033 close.
