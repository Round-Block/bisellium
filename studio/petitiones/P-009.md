Proposed amendment to the QA lex (`studio/leges/qa.md`), granting the
Censor the improvement-tracking obligation decreed in the W-035 plan
(approved 2026-09-20). Per QA §4 a lex-wording change is yours alone.

The architect reviewed the plan's draft text and corrected two clauses
before this reached you; the corrected text follows.

**§2 (Decides alone), add:**

> - Stamping `addressed_by` on a recurring class the Censor closes by
>   adopting an advisory rule alone (check: lesson.addressed_by)

**§3 (Digests), replace the recurrence bullet:**

> - A `class` of finding recurring across cascades; the advisory goes
>   quiet once some lesson of the class names what addresses it
>   (check: lesson.recurrent)

*(The plan's draft said "reportable once some lesson names what addresses
it" — inverted: the rule reports when nothing does and goes silent when
something does. As drafted it would have obliged the Censor to do the
opposite of what its own cited rule emits.)*

**§4 (Asks), add — deliberately without a `(check:)` marker:**

> - A recurring class the Censor cannot address alone — filed as a petitio
>   naming the proposed opus, rule, or acceptance decision. A class left
>   unaddressed at the close of the next cascade is an unmet obligation.

*(No rule enforces the one-cascade deadline, and W-035 deliberately builds
no time-based escalation — every time rule in this codebase is advisory.
Marking the clause with a rule id that does not enforce it would be caught
by `lex.marker_unknown`, the rule W-035 itself ships. So the clause goes in
unmarked and `lex.unchecked` will count it, correctly, until a deadline
rule exists or the clause is withdrawn. That trade is yours to accept or
reject.)*

**§7 (Evidence contract), add:**

> The Censor carries the addressed ledger forward: every retro's
> `## Addressed` section lists each recurrent class with its target, and a
> class recorded as formally accepted names the `decisions/D-nnn.md` that
> accepts it, with its `kill_when`.
