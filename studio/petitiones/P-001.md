Retrospectio 4b (acta/2026-09-18-retro-4b.md) found six failure classes
recurring across two or more cascades. Four of them need something I cannot
adopt alone — a blocking rule or a lex clause. Consolidated here as one ask;
the advisory rules I am adopting on my own authority are drafted as
D-008..D-011 and are not part of this petitio.

**1. Builder isolation (lex clause, leges/engineering.md).** Proposed
wording: *"Each builder in a cascade works in its own git worktree, or lands
a commit of its own files before the censor runs. A verifier's result is void
if the tree changed under it. The censor restores a file from a copy it made,
never with `git checkout --`."* Evidence: lessons/L-003.md, lessons/L-010.md.
Without this, the one-opus-owns-its-files clause already in
leges/engineering.md is unenforceable — cascade 4b produced at least eight
out-of-scope edits that cannot be attributed to any builder — `npm test` gave
two different answers one minute apart on the same commit, and the censor
protocol's own restore step destroyed uncommitted builder work.

**2. Officina bookkeeping is committed, not loose (new blocking rule,
`opus.untracked`).** Proposed: with `--repo <dir>`, `check` blocks when an
opus in `building`, `verifying` or `review` — or the `briefs/<id>.md` its
`spec:` key points at — is untracked in that repo. Evidence: lessons/L-006.md
and `git status` at HEAD 51fb2ba, where `studio/briefs/` and
`studio/opera/W-016.md .. W-019.md` are all `??`. The record of what was
asked and what each builder decided is not durable repo state today, so a
cold subagent cannot reconcile a brief against your actual words, and no
`tree:` certificate can be minted while that record sits loose in the working
tree. This is the root cause you asked about, and it is upstream of 1, 3 and 4.

**3. A behaviour without a red is not a behaviour (new blocking rule,
`opus.red_evidence`).** Proposed: an opus whose `spec:` brief lists numbered
behaviours must record in its body one captured failing output per behaviour
before its `tests` gate may be `passed`; a missing red blocks `verifying` →
`review` the way `state.building.spec` blocks `building`. Evidence:
lessons/L-001.md, lessons/L-008.md. D-006 already states the principle and
nothing can fail it: in cascade 4b, 28 behaviours across W-016 and W-018 were
filed with no red at all, and a builder report's `typecheckOk: true` stood
against a tree that exits 2.

**4. Ids are never joined into paths (promote D-008 to blocking after one
clean cascade).** Proposed: the containment contract `manifest.id.format`
states for manifest ids extends to every id that reaches a write — hook
payload `session_id`, `--kind`, sella and opus ids — with containment proven
by a resolved-path relation, never `startsWith`. Evidence: lessons/L-004.md,
lessons/L-011.md. I am adopting the detection as advisory now (D-008); I ask
that it become blocking once one cascade passes advisory-clean, because it is
the only class here that has been fixed twice and come back a third time.

If you would rather take these one at a time, 2 is the one to take first.
