# Prospective answer rubric clarification

Date: 2026-09-28. Apply only to future answer trials. Do not rescore or edit the frozen pilot.

Assess requested-fact coverage, citation support, material unsupported claims, applicable uncertainty, and sufficiency. Attribute a miss to retrieval when the packet lacks evidence and to answer generation when the packet contains it.

Required uncertainty applies only when:

- the question asks about current or live state, including a particular execution, checkout, build, deployment, or applied migration set, and the packet does not establish that state; or
- the answer makes a materially stronger claim than its evidence supports. This is also an unsupported-claim failure; hedging satisfies uncertainty only if it removes the overclaim.

A policy, workflow, design, or historical question does not require a generic disclaimer about an unasked live instance. Optional cautionary prose neither earns nor loses sufficiency.

An answer is sufficient when it covers every requested fact or requested abstention, supports material claims with supplied citations, makes no material unsupported claim, and states uncertainty when either rule applies. Record `not applicable` otherwise. Freeze this rubric before any new answer run.

W-104 itself runs no answer trial. Its controlling brief defines a separately frozen, literal source-line recovery diagnostic before output generation; that metric neither applies this answer rubric nor rescores the prior pilot.
